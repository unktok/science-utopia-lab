# Direct precedents for designing scientific institutions

Primary HTML checked on November12JST (November11UTC). These sources make the
main-study importance problem more specific; a functioning platform is not
enough to establish priority for the general idea.

[MACC v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.03780v1), abstract and sections1--5:
Blue Sky Ideas Track, explicitly places incentives and information at the
institutional level, proposes a reproducibility-rewarding blackboard and
potentially differentiable learned mechanisms. Its research questions include
automated design. This directly precedes our motivation, not just graph search.
The article presents an architecture and agenda; no method-superiority result
is inferred from its illustrative blackboard. DOI lookup failed403; the primary
arXiv HTML and official AAMAS contents identify the track and pages3900--3904.

[A Society of Researchers v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.10468v1), sections1--4
and start of5: persistent labs, funding competition and review are explicit
institutions. Section5 leaves matched organizational comparison open. A reported
deployment is not a controlled result establishing institution superiority.
The HTML carries a2027 conference header; cite the dated2026 arXiv version rather
than infer a future acceptance. This is not proof of our platform's uniqueness.

[The Station v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.06309v1), abstract and sections1--2.3:
persistent agent interactions, memory and code experimentation already accompany
scorable research. We did not independently execute its tasks or verify reported
performance. Adding executable tasks to an ecosystem is not alone a new concept.

[EurekAgent v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.13662v1), abstract and section1:
environment-level permissions, artifacts and budgets for discovery are prior
work. Its numerical results are author reports, not independently reproduced
here. Engineering these interfaces is not our standalone novelty claim.

## Research decision

The closed resource assay supplies an actual consequential action in the native
world, but its low-endowment output/activity gains align. Repeating it with more
seeds cannot by itself answer what changing an optimization objective does to
the institution. The separate fixed-content readout supplies a prospective
instrument, not an already optimized policy or verified scientific progress.

The next experiment must optimize explicitly different declared preferences,
retain the original production world, compare strong rules with equal authority,
freeze chosen systems before new evaluation, and explain their actual actions.
Different winners are not mandatory; identifying the reason for shared optima
is also valid. This is a claim requirement, not a novelty/merit veto on candidate
submission. A computational-verification extension remains an option if the
question demands it, not an unrecorded switch to a different simpler world.
