# Research logbook — 2026-10-31

## Metric-conditioned held-out study completed and released

The 15-row common held-out split completed at 05:59 JST with 15 complete rows,
zero failed rows, and no stale run. The launchd scheduler and watchdog removed
themselves after the result-blind readiness gate opened.

The commit-pinned controller generated `cross-evaluation.json`. The integration
publisher independently revalidated the freeze chain, all three optimization
registries, the complete 3-by-3 loss matrix, metric profiles, common-run
evaluations, and preregistered analyses. The first publication attempt exposed
a validator bug: terminal optimization failures were being treated as
unresolved even though the campaign state correctly retained them as
`observed_invalid` with no numeric loss. The validator now accepts only
attempt-exhausted terminal failures for optimization runtime accounting,
rejects retryable failures, and still requires every held-out row to be
complete.

The audit also corrected runtime accounting from final logical-row duration to
recorded attempt duration. The final package reports at least 52.35 hours and
one interrupted attempt without a recoverable wall-time value. It generated
three PDF/PNG figures, CSV tables, paired effects, compact JSON, Markdown,
LaTeX, and a hash manifest through one atomic output boundary.

The experimental result is two distinct frozen designs from three objectives,
a descriptive loss-dependent rank reversal, and no stable pairwise winner:
all nine paired held-out loss intervals contain zero. The participation/review
design produces 11.6 accepted papers and complete review coverage on average,
versus 4.0 and roughly 0.55 for the duplicated topic/burden design, while
incurring higher review burden. These are simulated diagnostics, not policy
recommendations.

## Working paper reframed around objective-conditioned search

The paper was audited against the original Suto paper to ensure that the Lab
does not merely repeat fixed intervention comparisons. The durable distinction
is now recorded in Decision 0020: the optimization objective is the
experimental intervention, while the search path and resulting institution are
the primary outcomes.

The manuscript was reorganized accordingly. Its title and abstract now center
objective-conditioned black-box optimization; the introduction contrasts
matched counterfactual intervention studies with sequential institutional
search; the main study defines the three LossSpecs and asks path, design, and
cross-objective ranking questions. The first engineering campaign and the game
observatory were moved to appendices so they support rather than dominate the
research claim.

The revision also records negative evidence in the main interpretation. Three
objective labels yielded two distinct designs, the topic and burden arms had
only two valid candidate systems, and no equal-budget optimizer baseline or
optimizer-seed replication has yet been run. The v1.1 result is therefore an
existence proof for objective-conditioned institutional search, not evidence of
optimizer efficiency, repeatable path divergence, structural optimization, or
real-world policy quality.

The revised LaTeX built successfully into an 11-page PDF. All pages were
rendered to PNG and inspected for clipping, overlap, figure legibility, and
section ordering. The paper-boundary regression affected by the revised flow
figure passed. In the repository-wide suite, 917 tests passed and 6 were
skipped; six remaining failures are in the external `ScienceUtopia`
prospective-logging tests, which currently monkeypatch a `subprocess` attribute
that the imported `funding_feedback` module no longer exposes. No failure
remains in the revised paper boundary.
