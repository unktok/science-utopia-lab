## Two-axis optimization continuum implemented

The apparent sequence from parameter optimization to structural optimization
to differentiable optimization was clarified into two axes. Parameters,
mechanism implementations, and node/edge topology are nested levels of design
freedom; black-box, differentiable-surrogate, and hybrid optimization are
proposal methods. Structural and differentiable therefore are not mutually
exclusive categories.

The first common implementation uses one six-family relaxed system template.
At the three executable freedom levels it exposes 6, 16, and 26 raw variables,
respectively. Gradient-free black-box proposals and freedom-masked gradient
proposals now cross the same deterministic projection, valid `SystemSpec`
fingerprint, declared loss, scenarios, and opaque outer evaluator. A hybrid
path uses a gradient result as the center of a result-dependent black-box
episode without treating surrogate score as simulator evidence.

The deterministic regression probe used eight gradient steps and four
black-box evaluations. At parameter, mechanism-configuration, and graph
freedom, black-box best outer losses were 1.133806, 1.133806, and 0.111956;
gradient projected outer losses were 1.133800, 1.133800, and 0.000056. A
three-evaluation structural hybrid retained 0.000056. These values are only
properties of the analytic synthetic fixture and do not rank real optimizers.

Thirty focused tests passed, including the existing differentiable and
black-box suites. The remaining integration gaps are schedule/latency in the
common relaxation and replacement of the synthetic evaluator with the compact
model-backed simulator. Decision 0022 records the architecture and claim
