## Program-model correction and organization

The next optimizer uses the supervised-learning distinction explicitly:

- `LossSpec` is the scalar or vector signal seen during optimization;
- `EvaluationMetricSpec` is analogous to held-out accuracy or calibration;
- `ValiditySpec` rejects corrupt or incomplete observations only;
- `DecisionPolicy` is optional post-evaluation governance and defaults to
  `report_only`.

The protected-slice veto belongs to the completed confirmation campaign. It is
not an implicit constraint on future optimization. The two funding-capacity
points were repeated evaluations, not a closed-loop search.

historical documents keep their existing paths. The raw confirmation artifacts
