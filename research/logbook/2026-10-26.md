## Compute planning and parallel tracks

The 10 completed four-year runs consumed 14.40 wall-clock hours. Their 3,310
LLM requests contained 3.54M prompt tokens and 1.98M completion tokens; LLM
elapsed time was 99.78% of total wall time. This establishes 38.24 effective
completion tokens/second and about 7.20 hours per five-seed design as the local
planning baseline.

Recorded a throughput-based GPU sizing table rather than treating a GPU name as
a runtime guarantee. A 24 GB NVIDIA GPU is the useful minimum for one Qwen3-8B
worker, 32-48 GB is preferred, and multiple GPUs should initially run independent
model replicas assigned distinct seeds/designs. Any CUDA backend or quantization
change begins a new producer configuration and requires calibration.

Long runs will use a clean, commit-pinned experiment worktree. Canonical event
and replay support, the game viewer, Evaluation System API, and the typed
mechanism graph can then proceed in separate worktrees without changing the
source or dirty-state recorded by an active campaign. Full calculations and
operational constraints are in `research/compute-and-parallelization-plan.md`.

## Confirmation protocol freeze

Defined confirmation protocol v1 before executing any of the untouched seeds
401, 409, 419, 421, and 431. The protocol separates technical inadmissibility
from institutional capability failure. The former makes a campaign incomplete;
the latter remains in the paired bundle and is summarized as a capability
outcome distribution and pass/fail transition.

The frozen candidate gate requires zero candidate capability failures, no
unknown capability state, no baseline-pass to candidate-fail transition, no
negative protected-slice participation pair, and a nonnegative mean aggregate
participation delta. Greater grant input forces a `confirmed_tradeoff` label
rather than dominance. Added separate baseline and candidate confirmation study
specifications and comparison schema v2 support. The existing exploration v1
JSON and Markdown regenerate byte-for-byte unchanged.
