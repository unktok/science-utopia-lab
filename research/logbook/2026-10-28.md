## Program-map reconciliation

The primary scientific question was restated as metric-conditioned system
optimization: change the optimization `LossSpec`, allow that change to alter
the search trajectory and resulting scientific system, then compare the frozen
systems on a common untouched evaluation suite. Metric separability between a
small number of hand-picked candidates is supporting QA, not the main result.

At 01:38 JST the frozen study had 27 initial logical runs registered across its
three arms. One of the eventual 45 optimization runs was complete, one was
running, and no held-out seed had been registered or consumed. Parallel
development foundations were also reconciled: whole-system `SystemSpec` and a
generic mechanism runner are integrated; the game has a verified Phaser v2
vertical slice with production pixel assets; the paper has a NeurIPS-style
working draft. Structural/topology optimization, differentiable optimization,
game interventions, and empirical metric-conditioned comparison results remain
future work.
