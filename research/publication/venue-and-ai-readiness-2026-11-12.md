# Venue preparation and AI disclosure November 12 2026

This is a read-only official-source check and our preparation assessment,
not a venue selection, acceptance prediction, legal clearance or submission.
The [November8 review](release-and-venue-review-v2.md) remains its historical
snapshot; its statement that2027 dates were unavailable is not current advice.

## Official current information

The [ICML2027 Dates page](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/Dates) now lists
January14,2027 AoE for abstracts and January22,2027 AoE for full papers;
submission/account preparation opens January8. Its
[CFP](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers) and
[Author Instructions](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorInstructions)
still say those documents will be posted. Dates being visible does not make
the old NeurIPS2025 style a2027-compliant submission template. Do not infer a
page limit or final checklist from last year's rules.

The [2027 AI policy](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/UseOfGenerativeAIByAuthors)
is accessible. It requires substantive AI use to be described in a disclosure
near the references, separate from sufficient methodological reporting of
experimental models. Human authors retain responsibility; an AI system cannot
be listed as an author. Routine formatting is distinct from research design,
code, analysis and substantial writing. Our use is substantive. This policy
does not certify our work or imply that human verification has occurred.

The [Contribution Types page](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2027/ContributionTypes)
requires one primary type, not a list of every project output. Concept and
Feasibility is not a preliminary-work exemption; Negative Results needs an
important explained finding, not merely an unsuccessful experiment. Datasets,
Evaluations and Benchmarks need not introduce a new learning algorithm, but
must enable a worthwhile evaluation question and support their stated scope.
These are contribution descriptions, not easier acceptance tracks.

The NeurIPS2027 CFP URL was inaccessible through this reader. That is an
access limitation, not proof that no official future policy exists. No further
venue timing or policy claim is inferred from it.

## Preparation decisions for this project

1. Keep the full reviewed integrated manuscript and all substantiated tracks.
   A later focused submission should organize, not erase, the broader evidence.
2. Do not sell existing program/graph search, manifests or gradient identities
   as a new optimizer. A central empirical question or evaluation resource
   must carry the contribution; the running pilot does not yet settle it.
3. Choose the contribution type from the actual scientific answer, not to
   excuse weak evidence. The implementation and isolated negative outcomes
   do not by themselves close R2/R3/R6.
4. Prepare an exact-file anonymous/reviewer package only after the main result
   and target rules stabilize. The previous12-page redaction/allowlist does
   not certify the61-page manuscript or new raw projections as anonymous.
5. Resolve human authors, contributions and confirmation before a submission.
   A non-blocking question requests candidates/affiliations; experiments continue.
   Redistribution rights, local35B provenance and fresh-machine limitations
   remain explicitly separate, with no new model download or public release.

## AI use draft pending human confirmation

Experimental roles include fixed local Qwen3-8B researchers, a separately
identified local35B designer, and model-based content opinions where that
study declares them. Their prompts, decoding, roles, failures and limitations
belong in the method, not only in an assistance statement.

Coding-agent assistance contributes to research formulation, experiment/control
design, implementation/debugging, data transformation and analysis, literature
discovery/comparison, scientific figures, and manuscript drafting/revision.
Automated source-bound tests, original-checkpoint audits, literal-record joins,
repeated exports and visual PDF checks verify particular properties and have
their own documented limits. They are not independent human peer review,
independent reproduction of all cited papers or proof of scientific truth.

Before publication, human authors must review the final claims and artifacts,
confirm their contributions and accept responsibility. Do not paste an example
statement claiming that all AI outputs were independently checked by the
authors without evidence/confirmation. This draft records known assistance
and outstanding sign-off rather than inventing either human authorship or
an already completed validation. Official policies must be checked again on
the exact submission version. No account change or external submission is made.
