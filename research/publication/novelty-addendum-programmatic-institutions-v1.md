Our inference from this comparison: reframe the prospective contribution around
an important identified finding about **objective-conditioned institutional
discovery in the persistent original researcher world**, not the claim that
institutions can be optimized or represented as executable programs. Concrete
graph/protocol freedom, fixed researcher weights, technical-null accounting and
frozen selection are study design choices and implementation capabilities.
They need scientific evidence, not a novelty claim assembled from capabilities.

The staged construction profile is a prerequisite for collecting meaningful
losses, not the central empirical answer. Its SCRIPTED success cannot show
that an LLM discovers a useful new institution, that different objectives
require different optima, or that greater representational freedom causes gains.
Preserve unfavorable outcomes and overlapping minima. Do not add a hidden
novelty/improvement constraint to force the desired conclusion.
