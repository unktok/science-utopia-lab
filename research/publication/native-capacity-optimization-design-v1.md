# Native capacity and objective conditioned institutional optimization

This is the next-study design, not a dispatched experiment or a change to the
live T/U study. Its question is whether a freely authored organizing system
changes the relationship between output and saved researcher activity under
an explicit native funding limit. It must optimize institutions, not merely
repeat a hand-selected institutional intervention at larger scale.

## Relation to the source paper and the local world

[ScienceUtopia §3.3 and Appendix C.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01257v1#S3.SS3)
compare one/two submissions per completed project under different funding
capacity regimes, reporting greater publication output alongside lower later
participation. They compare specified interventions rather than the generated
institutional programs proposed here. This targeted reading is not a fresh
replication of their large-world findings.

The local source must be audited before borrowing that interpretation. Its
native baseline has zero direct submission debit; the configured paper fee15
is unused in the historical per_paper flow. Annual native research cost is10.
Increasing papers_per_project therefore does not silently activate a new fee
or guarantee the source paper's trade-off. The fixed budget mode, already
implemented by the original Simulation, apportions round(fraction×initial
university population) annual award slots across programs. The current
five-researcher setting with fraction.2 gives one slot, not one per program.
Actual grants, costs, zero-clipping, state flags and project choices must still
be read from the native ledger and trajectories; a configured cap is not a
guarantee that it is used or that a particular trade-off appears.

## Prospective condition and two explicit objectives

Keep five original university Qwen3-8B researchers, other native agents,
the103107-record2016--2019 corpus, four native years, base30 balanced_two_band
resources, actual project durations and native costs. Do not replace them with
a numerical resource game. The primary pilot condition is papers_per_project2,
funding_budget_mode fixed and funding_budget_slots_frac.2. All algorithms in
that comparison receive the same condition; none may mint resources, replace
scientist weights or rewrite native production laws. Existing authored code,
entities, information routes, conserving transfers and institutional schedules
remain free within the declared native production/routing domain.

Use publication-count loss T=−accepted_decisions and the separate prospective
activity-state loss P=−active_founder_boundary_sum. P sums year-end author-capable,
is_active indicators for the actual initial cohort, excluding year0. New actors
do not inflate that cohort; an actually absent founder contributes zero by the
definition. Unknown flags or missing years do not acquire invented values.
With five initial authors/four years, its formal maximum is20, not a guarantee
of resource-feasible or scientifically meaningful participation.

P is not a constraint on T or U and is not a fairness/adoption veto. T-selected
systems may worsen P and vice versa. The exact native zero-resource activity
semantics remain visible: an active flag does not certify funds, useful work
or continued capability next year. Show actual research choices, project dates,
resources, receipts and outputs as outside diagnostics without rejecting a
valid low-loss system because those diagnostics are unattractive.

## Finite first comparison

The first pilot should contain two objective histories with two submitted
candidates each, one explicitly reused development seed61001 and a finite
same-authority reference pool. Keep every initial plan, source transaction,
execution/error, returned loss, full tie set, cost and realized graph change.
Do not require a new node, rewiring, different T/P winners or positive gain.

Reference candidates must include current reviewed accept-all/equalization,
its transfer cut and no-review accept-all/equalization, plus two explicit
conserving allocation algorithms with different information use: a pre-cost
reserve rule and a rule prioritizing original project/completion information.
Those are developer rules, not invented policies. A sixth early-information
reference may use the exact already declared advice regardless of whether its
pending timing assay gains or ties; no wording is selected from held-out scores.
Freeze the exact five/six systems and their tie rules before any new dispatch.
The upper bound is then six references+four candidate worlds, with no refill.
Do not add a weak initial threshold just to create an easy improvement.

The design role remains the approved local35B initially. Richer ABI documents
do not establish actual-model success. Run fresh source/model/backend-bound
readiness at the final source before dispatch, with finite call/output/read
allowances. A failed/unknown gate stops without repair/fallback or automatic
switch to a paid service. Scientist weights remain fixed in every case.

Final selection is among complete own literal submissions, including explicit
parent retention, with the earliest declared tie. No automatically substituted
reference when the submitted pool is empty. Keep all shared reference losses
visible and distinguish their use as an editing parent from final submission.
The main plan must declare and reconstruct all remaining exact transport,
component-test, world and cold-audit budgets; this memo is not that executable
plan and cannot authorize inference by itself.

## Follow-up decision and manuscript role

Close the existing641xx evaluation and the three-control timing assay normally,
including their original audits and actual input/project/loss readouts. Neither
is restarted, rescored or reselected for this new question. Inspect whether
information delivery and the resource state reach the intended native choices,
not just whether a code node executes. Then complete/freeze the finite pilot
above rather than running another unchanged T/U comparison.

If the pilot exposes a meaningful objective-to-institution distinction, use
its actual effect size, variation and measured cost to fix independent search
histories, unused seeds/conditions and strong reference comparisons. Fixed
policy evaluation and independent optimizer histories remain different units.
If it is flat or all failures, close that result with its causes/support; do
not expand the same study until it produces a preferred winner or silently
activate a per-paper expense to force a trade-off.

The substantive answer, not this design memo or implementation tests, must
support the main paper. Keep the full integrated manuscript and existing
gradient/free-source/UI tracks, but center a submission on what the actual
objective-to-system comparison establishes. Publication readiness stays open.
