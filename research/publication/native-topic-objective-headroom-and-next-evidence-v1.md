## Keep three distinct failure modes separate

1. **Conditional objective saturation.** A known rule may saturate a
   calendar or fixed-record class. This is not proof that the whole design
   language is exhausted or that a new objective should be fabricated.
2. **Incorrect actuation story.** In the [first own actual result](../results/native-topic-free-first-own-outcome-v1.json),
   the model's plan says the hardcoded accept-all selector relies on tensor
   scores; the literal consumer ignores that port. All179 full scientist
   requests/choices match its parent. This is observed-path equality, not
   global inertness of every memory branch or a causal account of model thought.
3. **Search effectiveness.** Valid source/graph submission, a passed directed
   control and a changed fingerprint do not show discovery of an operative
   or useful system. Conversely one tied first proposal does not prove that
   the remaining five slots or all local-model designers cannot improve.

Do not turn any of these into a scientific-merit veto. Preserve valid no-ops,
failures, adverse losses and original own-only selection. The current study's
remaining slots continue unchanged; per-request calibration remains distinct
from free-design capacity, and no independent heavy QA overlaps inference.

## What changes the publication decision after this study

First close the original eight-world maximum/six-slot history and paired
audit/cost/readouts. Then identify which objective-conditioned designs and
operative paths actually occurred. Do not automatically increase seed count
or repeat an unchanged search against a saturated target.

- An operative useful proposal requires its exact source, graph, actual
  consumed inputs/events and per-role costs. A claim that freedom or a method
  helps also needs a same-authority strong simple/parameter comparison;
  one improved developer condition is not independent method evidence.
- If all own changes remain nominal or tied, investigate the representation
  of actual consumers and objective-to-action understanding as a prospective
  intervention, without host repair or hiding the original failures. Do not
  call more interface tests the main scientific contribution.
- If claimed effects depend on a specific node, information route or timing,
  design the needed mechanism contrast before new inference. Exact existing
  input equality or fixed-content accounting is not causal mediation.
- Freeze selected systems and ties before unused-condition evaluation.
  Independent search histories are distinct from extra evaluation seeds,
  repeated files, author steps, content panels and component probes.

The original [ScienceUtopia v1 framework and experiments](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01257v1)
study longitudinal institutional interventions, research strategies and
feedback. Merely repeating its policy comparisons at larger scale would not
answer our objective-to-designed-system question. This is a scope distinction
from the inspected method/experiment descriptions, not a priority claim for
programmatic institution design or an assertion that the world validates real
science policy. A new top-conference claim needs an important supported answer
beyond elementary accept-all/shortest-duration incentives, generic LLM code
generation or faithful software execution. R2/R3/R5/R6 remain open.
