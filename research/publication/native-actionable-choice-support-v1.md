## Arithmetic is neither a feasible policy nor an acceptance optimum

For a hypothetical serial-project schedule, all founders active for four
years and two full outputs per project, `2 * sum(floor(4 / minimum_duration))`
is20 using these core minima and36 using the legacy unions. These numbers
ignore resource/cash-flow, production admission, collaboration, paper/venue
availability, retrieval, duplicates and model decisions. They are not measured
outputs or a global bound on native accepted-decision occurrences. In
particular, the hand control's observed20 does not establish a global optimum.
Other institutional/action paths and repeated decision accounting have not
been exhaustively bounded by this calculation.

The existing core assignment control's shortest projects therefore cannot
settle how much freedom the full information/planning system has. Conversely,
finding a one-year catalog label does not establish an implementable gain.
Funding and timing may become more consequential under frequent production;
that remains a hypothesis, not a new result or silent environmental change.
