## What this contrast can establish

The two policies are developer-authored, same-authority original-world systems.
Their one code difference acts after final publication/funding and before native
annual cost. This is a reachable-opportunity test, not automatic invention or
a controlled comparison of optimizers. It can contradict an interpretation that
the recorded strong parent is a world-level C optimum. It cannot demonstrate
a general improvement of scientific research, an optimizer advantage, recursive
self-improvement or a positive effect of automatically invented wiring.

The outcome need not be favorable. Keep missing losses unknown and keep ties,
worsening and unexpected content/input paths. No gain requirement or hidden
activity/unpaid-cost/fairness gate is introduced. Compare actual full scientist
requests and accepted year/paper/content records, not just equal totals. The
complete paths may differ after final cost; no future-year behavior is observed.

## Accounting decomposition, not a replacement loss

Let K be actually collected original activity costs and Q their unpaid amounts.
Original ledger transactions distinguish requested negative expense from actual
clipped debits. Requested activity expense R=K+Q is therefore a reportable
accounting diagnostic, not an independently validated measure of real resources.
Both K and Q exclude institutional income/transfer legs as before.

Keep the actual declared score literal:

    C_collected = L_U + 0.1 K

For interpretation only, report:

    D_requested = L_U + 0.1 (K + Q)
    delta D_requested = delta C_collected + 0.1 delta Q

`D_requested` is NOT optimized, not a new selected objective, not a veto on C
and not evidence that unpaid expenses ought to have any particular social value.
The identity separates content differences, collected charges and unpaid charges
within the observed records. If deltaC=-4 and deltaQ=40 while L_U and R are
unchanged, the entire score contrast is consistent with collection avoidance,
not reduced requested expense. That forecast is not substituted for observations.

No universal expectation, global frontier, causal attribution of LLM responses
or future-horizon benefit follows from a single paired condition. A final
participation/resource snapshot is not a long-horizon evaluation.

## Do not make this the entire main-track contribution

An endpoint accounting exploit is elementary on its own. A hand-written control,
larger report, exhaustive source hash list or green tests cannot replace the
original objective-to-designed-system research question. R2 importance, R3
adequate independent evidence and R5/R6 focused paper/final judgment remain open.

After the original two-world closure and interpretation, choose the next finite
comparison by the scientific question, not by whether this control wins:

- If the original objective opportunity is real, distinguish discovering an
  operative mechanism from editing a valid but unused parameter. A prospective
  author-information intervention can expose actual producer/consumer and
  native-cost semantics without inserting the terminal solution, restricting
  allowed designs, repairing proposals or requiring novel topology.
- A claim that such grounding improves search needs the same design authority,
  models, initial bank, output/call/transport budgets and within-history feedback
  across conditions, plus separately declared independent search histories.
  Old studies with different interfaces are not matched replications.
- Include strong simple controls, including any newly known developer policy,
  with its visibility declared before dispatch. Finding a known comparator's
  behavior is not beating it. Distinct objectives need not select distinct
  systems, and changed code does not imply a changed native trajectory.
- Freeze selection and ties before new unused-condition evaluation; extra
  evaluation seeds do not create independent optimization runs. Do not search
  evaluation records or reuse old reserved conditions as fresh.
- More free-system activity (new rules/mechanisms/processes/wiring) is a design
  freedom to test, not a mandatory novelty gate or an excuse to replace the
  original researcher world with an easier numerical substitute.

## Focused primary-source check during this wait

Re-inspected the following primary HTML sources on November16. This is a scoped
comparison, not an exhaustive priority search or a new experimental finding.

[AMD-LLM, sections3.1 and5.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.12203v1)
already generates/evolves mechanism code, uses problem-specific fixing for
declared design requirements, and compares generated heuristics with other
methods. Code-defined institutions and a propose/evaluate/revise loop are
therefore not our standalone novelty claim. Our preservation of literal failed
programs differs in protocol but does not itself prove a better search method.

[Inefficiencies of Meta Agents for Agent Design, sections3--4](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.06711v1)
already studies history/context curation, behavioral redundancy and design cost,
including repeated searches. Simply finding low diversity or expensive local
revision in our native world would not establish a new general phenomenon.
Observed literal input/choice classes are useful measurements, not a uniquely
identified cause of this designer's behavior.

[SciMARS, sections3.2--3.7 and4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.12552v1)
already adapts roles/coordination and, in its memory variant, evidence policy,
using candidate probes, composition and selection in scientific/interactive
tasks. An evidence-routing intervention cannot be called new merely because
it supplies feedback or changes an executable harness. The candidate distinction
here is optimizing the organizing system of persistent researchers under
declared institutional losses; that scope still needs an important supported
answer, not just a different setting or a novelty assertion.
