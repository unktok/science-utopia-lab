## Research branches, not candidate promotion criteria

If a generated rule actually improves a declared loss, identify its operative
change and compare against the strongest simple same-authority implementation.
Then choose a separately declared minimal mechanism contrast: preserved-effect
reimplementation, information/path removal or timing/state intervention, as
needed for the particular claim. Arbitrary code can implement a graph's
computation: new wiring is not inherently more expressive than general programs.
Do not attribute a code/algorithm/timing package to graph syntax alone.

If gains are only collected-cost avoidance, keep them as valid C optimization
without imposing an unpaid/fairness veto. The result cannot establish reduced
real work or better scientific knowledge. Its importance must come from a new
identified observation about proxy/institution interaction, not restating the
known hand-coded reference's arithmetic. Any better-motivated future objective
needs its own meaning/protocol before optimization; current U/C are not rewritten.

If corrected free search remains tied/inert/adverse, report that finite outcome
and inspect the specific separation among interface behavior, reachable policy
effects, proposal dynamics and objective headroom. An adequately active initial
rank kernel removes one known comparator defect; it does not prove effective
exploration everywhere, exhaust the native policy class or identify model
capacity as the remaining cause. Do not automatically grow seeds or add a
mandatory novel node. A causally discriminating follow-up is chosen only from
what the full original traces actually support, with a separate finite protocol.

Either sign of result can motivate useful research. Neither a single win nor
a sound null automatically closes significance. The main paper must explain
something important beyond existing automatic mechanism/program search and
persistent-agent institution simulation, with evidence adequate to its scope.
