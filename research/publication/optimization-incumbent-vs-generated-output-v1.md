# Optimization incumbents and generated submissions are different readouts

The research goal is minimizing an explicit loss over institutional systems,
not requiring a new program to replace an already better known system. Keep
two views in analysis and the manuscript; neither is a new loss or merit veto.

For completed common references R and the first g consumed own proposal slots,
define the best-known loss K_g as the minimum over R and completed own outcomes.
Define O_g as the minimum over completed own outcomes only; if none completes,
O_g is unavailable, not a penalty and not the value of a substituted reference.
Technical failures stay in the trajectory. Reference-first chronological tie
handling can identify a deterministic K incumbent while retaining every tie.

K is the relevant best-known optimization performance. O, literal edits and
executed effects answer what the generated proposals achieved. If O is worse
than a known reference, do not describe that worse value as if the optimizer
had forgotten its measured incumbent. Conversely, a good K inherited from a
developer reference is not an autonomous invention, generated improvement,
new graph or evidence that feedback helped. Report both rather than choosing
whichever presentation looks stronger.

## Preserve each original experiment

The closed planning pilot and ongoing presentation/numeric protocols explicitly
freeze own-only final selections. Keep those files, values, ties, nulls and
their meaning unchanged. A derived K readout does not rewrite their selections,
repair an unsuccessful proposal or relabel a reference as a generated candidate.
Other historical studies that explicitly allowed the initial system retain
their different declared rules; do not silently harmonize them.

The current [joint evaluation](../components/native-joint-fixed-evaluation-v1.md)
already includes every common reference and each original best-own selection.
A development-only K incumbent is necessarily one of those programs: a best
reference, or a best-own selection if its loss is strictly lower. Therefore
its fixed-policy outside scores can be read from existing physical evaluations
without another native world. Keep any incumbent alias/provenance separate
from missing-own labels; do not fill a missing proposal with its reference.
An evaluation outcome may never decide which development incumbent to use.
The prospective rule is recorded before any joint evaluation seeds/outcomes;
the current joint runner/readout still materializes the original16 labels,
not a new set of eight incumbent aliases.
