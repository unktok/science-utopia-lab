# What the workflow pilot can contribute, and what it cannot

November14 research-design update while the finite numeric experiment is live.
It changes no loss, proposal budget, current source or selection policy. A
publishable main result remains unproved. The previous goal turn was progress:
new manuscript source, actual partial input-class evidence and a live deferred
native-QA process, not merely a restatement of status.

## Candidate empirical question, not a promised answer

**What changes in a persistent scientist world when the optimizer can change
institutional observation/action timing, rather than only numerical settings,
and how do the declared objectives shape the returned institutions and paths?**

This is narrower than the long-term whole-system design space, but it asks
about an actually missing operated axis instead of enlarging the previous
source-only, flat-loss search. The original scientist/corpus/effects remain
present. The current reserve diagnostic identifies a real consumer and a
nonmonotone conditional surface; it does not identify a full-world optimum.
The numeric first T0/T1 cases have identical133 full inputs/reply choices and
zero actual transfers despite different parameter values. That is an observed
neighborhood, not proof that parameters can never change scientific behavior.

The new workflow protocol keeps sources/parameters fixed and frees existing
phase/typed-edge changes, with fresh R7 and a strong hand timing reference.
The general v4 runtime also permits other code/nodes; the structural-only study
is one controlled slice, not the ceiling of scientific-system abstraction.

Important confounds stay explicit. The numeric T anchor is advisory/reviewed
R2 while U uses assignment/no-review R7; the workflow study uses shared R7.
The interfaces and their designer access also differ. A native implementation
identifier is not every original Simulation method body, and developer
controls can use prior source/trajectory knowledge. These studies cannot by
themselves identify an isolated causal benefit of graph syntax or an equal-
information optimizer ranking. Loss-conditioned parent choice is part of the
end-to-end procedure, not proof that the generated edit caused the difference.

## Decisions after the finite pilot closes

1. **A generated workflow beats both fresh controls.** Retain the whole exact
   graph and all costs/nulls. Trace actual native effects, information delivered
   and annual resource/participation/production paths. Freeze the selection
   before unused conditions. Design a minimal, explicit mechanism followup
   only after the observed graph identifies what should be isolated. A one-seed
   gain alone is not stable superiority or a main-track ready result.
2. **Generated policies match or lose to a hand timing control.** Report the
   best-known and best-own paths separately. A useful developer timing effect
   is not automatically autonomous invention. Do not refill the run, require
   novel wires, weaken references or optimize a new proxy just to obtain a win.
   Decide whether the observed timing/feedback mechanism answers a sufficiently
   important question before paying for independent larger comparisons.
3. **All timing policies are practically tied here.** Close this condition as
   an exercised but unrewarding neighborhood. A negative claim needs evidence
   about the tested authority and observability, not a global impossibility
   claim. More repetitions of the same weak question do not close R2.
4. **Author/interface/reference execution stops.** Preserve the exact technical
   boundary and unknown costs, not an invented scientific loss or a conclusion
   that institutions cannot improve. Verify/fix genuine infrastructure errors
   in a new frozen source without repairing or refilling authored candidates.

These are interpretation branches, not merit/promotion gates inside the loss.
Original T/U, technical-null rules and finite budgets remain literal. No actual
workflow policy, expected improvement, independent optimization replication,
recursive inventor improvement or publication readiness is established here.

## Necessary next evidence, regardless of a favorable pilot

The numerical/structural comparison needs all original cold audits, complete
candidate paths, source/graph/consumer/request differences and real costs.
Independent optimizer histories and unused-condition evaluations are different
units; choose a finite main comparison from meaningful effects, observed
variation and feasible expense, not from a desire for significance. The main
paper must state one important supported answer and its limits. The broader
integrated manuscript keeps the other genuine research tracks without counting
their heterogeneous software checks as independent empirical support.
