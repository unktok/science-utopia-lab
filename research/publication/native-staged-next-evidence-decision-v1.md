## Why another identical search is not the next question

The [complete actual staged development](../results/native-open-staged-institution-closed-v1.md)
now has six measured own policies instead of five technical nulls. It shows
an operated agenda change that worsens utility, five ties, and no added node
or wire. Successful construction is therefore no longer the only unresolved
boundary. The study still does not identify why useful institution discovery
stagnates: source understanding, longitudinal observation access, reachable
support and search behavior remain competing explanations.

The inspected primary precedents constrain what this project can claim:

- [ADAS v1, sections3--4](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.08435v1): executable
  agent designs and archive-based meta search are already established.
- [AMD-LLM v1, section3.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.12203v1): code-defined
  mechanisms, evaluation-driven evolution and problem-specific fixing are prior
  art. Our refusal to repair candidates is a study choice, not superiority.
- [El et al. v1, sections3--4](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.06711v1): archive
  curation, behavioral redundancy and design economics are already studied.
  The evaluation objects there are code-defined question-answering agents,
  unlike this persistent research society. That difference is not a priority
  proof or a diagnosis of our failures.
- [Science Utopia v1, sections2.2/F.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01257v1):
  persistent scientific feedback and the default annual pipeline are inherited.
  Repeating funding/attrition interventions is not this project's new answer.

This is a targeted comparison, not an exhaustive novelty search or independent
reproduction of those studies. In particular, do not sell low behavioral
diversity, failure logging, Python institutions or an archive as our invention.
