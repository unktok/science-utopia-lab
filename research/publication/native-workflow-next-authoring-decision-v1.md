## What the observations change

1. JSON format alone does not solve semantic graph validity on the observed
   failed-history task. Both thinking arms can return a valid directed control.
   That is not a generally reliable free designer or proof of grammar enforcement.
2.8192 output tokens end before a free final answer. A one-shot16384 allowance
   does produce final JSON after12563 tokens, preserving the earlier thinking
   as an exact prefix. More output capacity solves finalization here, not
   causality: selection is put at40 before four producers at70/100/110/120.
   Do not increase the allowance further on this frame under these protocols.
3. The actual free frame's selected parent is the already stronger hand timing
   control, not the initial R7 graph. Explicit empty edits would legally retain
   it; nonempty edits and novelty are never required. The author's retention/
   optimality prose does not describe the submitted phase changes or prove loss.
4. The generic compiler error names a causal rule but not the offending edges.
   We can improve feedback without modifying the compiler or repairing a graph.

## Next method, before scientific dispatch

Use a finite compiler-feedback proposal history, not further blind one-shot
budget expansion. Each new returned proposal is a consumed slot. A known
invalid submission remains a null with its exact error/witnesses; its feedback
can inform a later newly identified proposal. Do not overwrite/relabel an old
slot, silently repair an edge, refill a failed slot, attach a loss to an
unexecuted graph, or demand improvement/novelty to continue.

Implement and verify the complete new source-bound production path: explicit
thinking/output/context settings, actual calibration public return/cold reader,
next feedback-bearing prompt, bounded worker, original native role factory,
loss observation, null handling, normal closure/audit/cost/report and fixed
selection handoff. Existing legacy per-request certificates and stopped-study
criteria retain their original meaning. If adopting a different campaign-wide
calibration scope, define and test that new scope explicitly; a copied control
is still not a guarantee about future free proposals or context capacity.
Deep-history input plus16384 output may not fit40960, so measure and declare
the complete context/output contract prospectively rather than truncate history
or redispatch an oversized call. No new model/service/payment is authorized.

The next scientific protocol should keep the two strong controls, original
T/U, fixed scientists/corpus/native effects and a finite small proposal/world
budget, with no automatic inference until the new path is fully verified.
Normally closed literal own selection must precede unused-condition evaluation.
Completed-control partial reports do not become a normal closure prerequisite.

This structural-only debugging slice is not the project's full design goal.
Free mechanism code/composition, other optimization methods and the common
observatory remain part of the program. An important main-track result still
requires an actually operated objective-to-system comparison and independent/
unused validation as appropriate, not another large count of interface tests.
