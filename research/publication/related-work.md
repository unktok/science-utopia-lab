# Initial related-work positioning

Checked2026-11-07. This is a primary-source scoping note, not an exhaustive
novelty review. Abstracts/official author explanations establish the distinctions
below; full-text comparison is still required before submission claims.
The subsequent [targeted method-section comparison](method-section-comparison.md)
adds primary-text reading of AMD-LLM, SGA and ScienceUtopia, without claiming an
exhaustive review.
Its follow-up also inspects the method sections of SocialSystemArena and
PolicySim, previously recorded only as abstract-level leads. The exact scope
and implications are retained there; neither is a verified reproduction.

| Source | Relevant prior contribution | Consequence for this project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Science Utopia](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01257),2026 | Persistent LLM researcher ecosystem and controlled institutional interventions | Credit/reuse the world. Our candidate contribution is searching organizing systems conditional on objectives, not another resubmission/funding sweep |
| [The AI Economist](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.13332),2020 | Two-level learned tax-policy design in a simulated economy | Simulated social-system optimization is not new. Distinguish fixed researcher weights, editable institution programs and scientific-domain evaluation |
| [Democratic AI](https://deepmind.google/blog/human-centred-mechanism-design-with-democratic-ai/),2022 | Human-preference-based mechanism design in a public-goods setting | Explicit objectives and stakeholders matter; do not infer human preference alignment from our proxy scores |
| [OPRO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.03409),2023/ICLR2024 | LLM proposals using prior solutions and evaluated values | The propose/evaluate/history loop is not itself a new optimizer |
| [FunSearch](https://github.com/google-deepmind/funsearch),2023/2024 | LLM program search paired with an evaluator | Executable-code evolution is established prior art; show the value and difficulty of the institution/world design space |
| [Interpretable LLM mechanism design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12203),2025 | Mechanisms as generated code, evolved against metrics, with problem-specific fixing | Neither code-as-mechanism nor LLM-generated rules alone is novel. Our whole-system wiring/process freedom and no silent policy repair require direct comparison |
| [ADAS / Meta Agent Search](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.08435v2#S3),2024/2025 | Code-defined agents/workflows; archive-conditioned generation and validation feedback | Free code/graph search and the space/algorithm/evaluator distinction are established. Do not claim our graph interface is inherently more expressive than general program synthesis |
| [LLM Economist](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.15815v1#S3),2025 | In-context planner optimizes a bounded marginal-tax vector over adapting LLM workers | Fixed model weights and LLM social-policy optimization are not new. Contrast numeric tax actions with our institution code/process domain, without claiming a new generic optimizer |
| [AgentSociety2](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.11895v2#S3.SS2.SSS3),2026 | Composable social environments and specification-to-code generation, validated before registration | Integration, executable social research and generated environment modules are prior art. Our study must substantiate objective/design-freedom consequences in a persistent research ecosystem |

These are differences to investigate, not proof that our combination is unique.
Additional work must cover institutional/social simulation, automated mechanism
and algorithm design, LLM multi-agent policy optimization, gradient estimation,
and science-of-science evaluation. Use the existing
[science-of-science notes](../literature/science-of-science-objectives.md) and
[internal survey notes](../literature/ai-scientist-survey-objectives.md) rather
than inventing a universal scientific-value objective.

Do not count debug iterations, total test count or a game screenshot as a new
scientific method. Do not position this as policy advice without external validity.
