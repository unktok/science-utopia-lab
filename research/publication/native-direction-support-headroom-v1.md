# Direction support: do not confuse the catalog control's20 with a whole-domain ceiling

Read-only analysis while the original staged experiment remains live. No
scientist, designer, current rule, reference, loss or finite budget is changed.
This note does not inject a host-authored winning rule into the actual author.

## The two real interfaces have different topic support

`native_catalog_planning.validate_catalog_assignment` accepts only a topic in
the real actor's displayed expertise, with exact catalog content/duration.
In contrast, original `create_research_directions_batch` in non-strict legacy
mode allows the displayed expertise **plus its direct related topics**, then
resolves the selected topic through `DIRECTIONS_DICT`. These are not identical
action sets. Missing catalog strings must not be renamed or invented silently.

The actual original seed61001 year0 R7 checkpoint has five active idle authors,
no paper backlog and a declared cap of two outputs/project over four years.
A read-only initial catalog/AST scan gives the following duration-only values:

| Original author suffix | Core-only minimum years | Legacy core+related minimum years | Examples of shortest legacy topics |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 0000 | 2 | 1 | neural_symbolic_ai |
| 0001 | 2 | 1 | artificial_intelligence, neural_symbolic_ai |
| 0002 | 2 | 2 | computational_science, performance_analysis, others |
| 0003 | 2 | 1 | deep_learning, meta-learning |
| 0004 | 2 | 1 | information_retrieval |

Thus `2 * sum(floor(4 / minimum_years_i))` is20 for the core-only support and36
for the legacy parser's support. These are **conditional initial-support
relaxations**, not two observed outcomes or an optimum proof. In particular,
36 ignores funding, attrition, actual LLM choices, retrieval, collaboration,
content opinions and whether the desired serial path can actually be induced.
Additional assumptions include no new native authors/future expertise changes,
serial noninterrupted projects, the stated output cap and at most one accepted
decision per real produced output. The arithmetic reader does not automatically
prove these assumptions for every possible legal graph/trajectory.

The earlier20 envelope was explicitly about catalog projects, and remains true
under its stated assumptions. It must not be promoted to a global impossibility
claim about the broader workflow. Nor does36 establish an eight/sixteen-paper
attainable improvement, a constructed successful institution or scientific merit.

## Source and consumer checks

The workflow-v4 guard fixes native production/effect nodes and the real plan/
paper input origins, forbids extra native research-production delegates and
arbitrary `native.project` authority, but allows institutional source/nodes/
wiring and ordinary addressed context. Generic `actor.create` creates custom
domain actors, not new original native author objects. Catalog assignment keeps
ongoing work and native durations intact. Original native direction parsing can
still be reached if an institution does not preempt every idle actor's choice
with a direct catalog assignment.

The original direction prompt calls the researcher's personality renderer;
the researcher subclasses append `institutional_context_prompt`. Therefore
stored context has an actual route to that native decision, rather than merely
an unused parameter name. That source path is not evidence that a particular
generated advice message has been delivered, obeyed or improved a loss.
Actual later input/choice/effect readouts must establish those observations.

The new `native_direction_support_v1` reader parses the frozen catalog with AST
literal evaluation only, binds the actual year0 R7 policy/checkpoint and plan,
reports each exact support/minimum and the explicit assumptions, and checks
bound input bytes before/after. It executes no source, world or model. Its
sealed actual export is now [retained unchanged](../data/native-direction-support-v1/report.json):
two actual source-pinned exports60086/35971 exit0 and are byte-identical.
This binds initial support and arithmetic, not future attainability or a full
original-source native-world audit. The reviewed82-page PDF remains its earlier
snapshot; this subsequent analysis has not yet been integrated into that PDF.

## Research decision, without changing the active experiment

Continue the original six-slot search and planned selection-fixed evaluation;
do not change the loss, insert another control, refill failures or feed this
post-hoc host analysis into its fixed optimizer context. Twenty is not a reason
to declare automatic improvement impossible or close the broad research goal.

If useful own variation remains absent, a finite prospective next mechanism
test can ask whether **direct assignment versus addressed advice operates
different native choice support**, with matched timing/resources and unchanged
scientists/production. A related-topic advice rule would be a new separately
labelled developer control, not an automatically invented institution. Compare
same-actuation alternatives before claiming an isolated support effect. No such
new rule/world is implemented or dispatched by this note, and a positive result
would still need appropriate independent evidence before a main-track claim.

This particular support mismatch is a property of the inherited simulator and
its catalog-command adapter, not by itself an important new general result
about scientific institutions. Use it to avoid a false saturation explanation
and design discriminating controls, not to replace the publication goal with
a small arithmetic/software-interface observation.
