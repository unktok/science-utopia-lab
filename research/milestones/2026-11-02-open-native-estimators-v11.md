# V11 finite goal: open native institutions with optional gradient estimators

Status: active development, no v11 native result yet.

## User intent and scope

Optimize the scientific organizing system, not differentiability itself.
Changes may include implementation code, rules, communication, wiring and
processes, not just a review scalar. Keep the original researcher world and
fixed Qwen3:8B weights. Do not scale population, years or seeds. Prior v8/v10
evidence remains historical and immutable.

## Parallel work and completion evidence

| Work | Finite deliverable | What does not count |

| Documentation | User clarification, estimator references, exact result/remaining limits, updated program map and manuscript source | Declaring every future pre-scale research question solved |

## Operational next step

The [prospective native pilot](../protocols/campaigns/native-open-estimators-v11.md)
uses one existing utility objective, two methods, two authoring generations and
two IID draws per generation: eight native attempts maximum. First generation
definitions are method-matched; later source revisions may use only their own
observed history. This tests an executable integration, not optimizer
superiority. The estimator is conditional on the generated vocabulary, not a
gradient of the external source author's unknown distribution.
