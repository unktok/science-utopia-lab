## Frozen execution scope

The [prospective native pilot](../protocols/campaigns/native-open-estimators-v11.md)
uses one existing utility objective, two methods, two authoring generations and
two IID draws per generation: eight native attempts maximum. First generation
definitions are method-matched; later source revisions may use only their own
observed history. This tests an executable integration, not optimizer
superiority. The estimator is conditional on the generated vocabulary, not a
gradient of the external source author's unknown distribution.

## Remaining, without a scale-up prerequisite

The G0 plain-LR proposal update is followed by native G1 evaluations. The G1
corrected-control update is saved but not subsequently evaluated. A follow-on
pilot can test that distribution without increasing population, horizon or
seed count. This budget is now closed; no extra draw is silently appended.
Estimator variance/advantage, autonomous useful source invention, better
designer strategies and stronger scientific objectives remain empirical
research questions, not hidden completion gates. The generic code/graph API is
broader than this pilot's two authored algorithms and cutoff family.
