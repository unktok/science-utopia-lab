## Acceptance gates

| Gate | Required implementation and small-scale evidence | Not implied |
|---|---|---|
| Open native activity | Typed plans/paper bundles expose private annual-frame dependencies; arbitrary connected institution code changes an actual research action; original six-year scientific-phase equivalence retained | Unlimited new actor ontology or a scientifically realistic world |
| Design-distribution backward | Factorized arbitrary code/parameter/wiring choices have exact joint log probabilities; full native costs update their policy; analytic/finite-difference and restart checks | Pathwise derivatives through the complete native world or frozen LLM responses |
| Persistent design lifecycle | Real boundary measurement → exact authored proposal → explicit durable adoption in the same society → later outcome supplied to subsequent design; cold restart and interrupted acknowledgement recover | Causal superiority from before/after observations, or a designer improving its own algorithm |
| Game-like research interface | English existing cyberpunk UI shows actual loss-specific paths, every generated graph/code revision and explicit adoption state; unavailable operations stay unavailable | A full management game or fabricated successful adoption |
| Integration and evidence | Independent review, regression checks, execution/source hashes, failure records, updated notes and working paper | Scientific-value discovery, scalability or generalization |

These gates close executable interfaces and evidence gaps; they do not turn open
research questions into achieved results. Any remaining non-scale limitation
must be recorded explicitly, not hidden behind a milestone label. A longer
small-world horizon is allowed only where causally necessary to observe an
institution's subsequent effect, not to improve statistical significance.

stocks, separate from complete-run measurements. Its journal stages the exact
proposal before installing it, binds explicit confirmation to the complete
