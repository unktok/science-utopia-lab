## Boundaries after this finite slice

- Native capability interfaces are extensible, but new privileged world laws
  still require trusted implementations; generated code has no arbitrary host access.
- Source/structure design is external Codex authorship. More favorable sampled
  institutions do not establish an endogenously self-improving inventor.
- Two-draw LR estimates and one same-seed development followup per arm do not
  establish expected improvement, variance reduction or generalization.
- The illustrative losses are explicit engineering proxies, not an agreed
  scientific or national-policy welfare function. No unrequested veto is added.
- Whole-world pathwise differentiation is neither implemented nor required.
  Suitable represented derivatives and expected-loss estimators remain distinct.
- The English cyberpunk interface is an auditable game-like workbench, not
  a finished management game. Local editing does not dispatch or adopt a system.
