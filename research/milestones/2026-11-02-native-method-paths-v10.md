## Remaining scope limits

- One observed pre-intervention history, one scalar institutional degree of
  freedom and three proposals per method: no independent-history uncertainty,
  held-out evidence, global optimum or optimizer-superiority conclusion.
- The real native parser and objective remain unchanged. Derivatives are of
  an explicit response representation, not the exact Qwen/text/world Jacobian.
- Different theta outcomes, even near each other, may include Qwen stochastic
  variation. This pilot cannot isolate a causal policy benefit.
- A fresh point that ties a known historical best is not a new discovery.
  Moving the current coordinate is not itself evidence of improvement.
- This finite implementation/experiment goal is not completion of every
  pre-scale research question, useful automatic invention or recursive
  designer improvement.
