# Milestone 2 — Untouched-seed confirmation of the first candidate

**Date:** 2026-10-27  
**Outcome:** the five-seed confirmation campaign completed; the funding-capacity
candidate was **not confirmed** under the frozen distributional rule.

## What is now complete

ScienceUtopia can now carry one institutional candidate through the full research
cycle:

1. freeze a constitution, metrics, slices, and decision policy;
2. select a candidate on exploration evidence;
3. preregister how institutional capability failures are retained;
4. execute untouched paired confirmation worlds;
5. audit provenance, technical integrity, and funding conservation;
6. apply noncompensable gates without replacing adverse worlds.

The 10 confirmation runs were produced from one clean commit with the same local
Qwen3-8B model, dataset, evaluator, and four-year fidelity. Every run completed
on its first harness attempt and passed technical checks.

## Scientific result

Raising the annual fixed funding-capacity fraction from 0.3 to 0.5 yielded:

- mean aggregate participation delta +0.06;
- mean low-resource participation delta +0.12;
- one baseline capability failure repaired (seed 401);
- no candidate capability failures;
- one protected-slice reversal (seed 431, 0.8 → 0.6);
- grant income +160 and funding awards +8 in every paired world.

The reversal violates the preregistered requirement of no negative
low-resource participation pair. The candidate is therefore `not_confirmed`.
This demonstrates why the project keeps capability and distributional gates
outside average scalar compensation: a favorable mean did not erase harm in one
protected world.

## Meaning for the optimization program

The first optimization loop did not end with a winning policy; it ended with a
valid falsification of the fixed candidate's robustness claim. That is a useful
research result and a successful test of the governance machinery.

Future work should expand from a single parameter intervention to a newly frozen
multi-design campaign, using the observed heterogeneity to formulate hypotheses
about mechanisms. It must allocate fresh seeds and preserve this comparison as a
closed confirmation record.

## Engineering result

The comparison command now derives the requested role's seed bundle from both
study snapshots. This closes a CLI bug that otherwise made confirmation reports
attempt to use exploration seeds. The result is covered by a regression test.
