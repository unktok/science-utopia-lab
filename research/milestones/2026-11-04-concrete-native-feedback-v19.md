# Both objective native feedback loops verified

The finite goal is now supported at a single frozen implementation: local
Qwen3-8B submits a system, the original researcher world measures its loss,
the next local-model generation receives that exact own result, and another
native world measures the submitted system. This holds for both objectives.
Retention was permitted, not replaced by a novelty or improvement condition.

The [actual result](../results/native-concrete-closed-loop-v19-a1.md) records
throughput losses `[-1,-1]` and reviewed-production losses `[8,8]`. All four
systems retain the parent. The result verifies an operational loop, not useful
new institutions, improved science or effective objective-dependent redesign.

## Preserved failures and remaining research

V17's 12 charged slots and failed outcomes remain unchanged. V18's diagnostics
remain zero-inference archive analyses. V19 is a separately budgeted engineering
continuation, not a controlled interface comparison. Its seven rejected edits
are not hidden by the zero generation/native-failure count. All eight attempted
edit operands equal their parent values, so rejecting extra metadata did not
conceal an improved source in this sample.

The next research problem is obtaining and evaluating genuinely different,
valid institutional designs under the selected losses. More seeds or larger
worlds would not establish this missing capability by themselves. Arbitrary
code, institutions, schemas, wiring and processes remain supported; the local
model did not use that freedom effectively here. The separate Codex-designer
choice remains unanswered, not assumed. Known unused-path contract caveats
and reliable invention remain open work, not part of the achieved loop claim.

No owned inference/test writer remains after the finite run and verification.
The user-owned local backend is untouched. Code and records are integrated
into local main only; no remote push, paper/PDF or game update is claimed.
