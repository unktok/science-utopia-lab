# 0028 — Gradient estimators serve open institutional optimization

Date: 2026-11-02. Explicit user clarification after v10: differentiability is
not the research objective or a mandatory completion gate. The objective is to
optimize freely designed scientific institutions under an explicitly chosen
loss, using useful gradients, gradient estimators or black-box observations.

## Two independent axes

The design may change parameters, algorithms/source, mechanisms, protocols,
connections and processes. The search method may use derivative-free proposals,
likelihood-ratio estimators, represented pathwise gradients or a justified
combination. Neither axis restricts the other to a developer's finite catalog.
Fixed researcher-model weights do not mean fixed researcher responses.

Keep the original researcher world as an executable contained case and extend
it. Do not replace that world with a numeric miniature to make gradients easier.
All proposed institutions are evaluated by the same declared native LossSpec.
There is no implicit quality, fairness, participation or promotion veto.

## What a gradient claim means

1. A derivative of a hard/soft representation is a derivative of that
   representation, not automatically of the native world.
2. A likelihood-ratio estimate can optimize an expected native loss without a
   derivative of the environment, when the relevant sampling probabilities,
   support and integrability conditions permit the identity.
3. Our generated-vocabulary likelihood is conditional on the declared version;
   it is not a likelihood of arbitrary code invention or opaque researcher text.
4. If a frozen surrogate is used as a control variate, retain the expectation
   correction. Subtracting its prediction alone can change the expected update.
   Train it on eligible prior data, not the same current samples being scored.
5. Mixing a biased approximate-world derivative with a native score estimate
   does not automatically remove bias. Label the estimand and assumptions.

Parmas and collaborators' unified LR/RP view, composite estimators and
propagator abstractions are relevant method references, not methods we may
claim to have implemented merely by citing them. See the
[literature note](../literature/parmas-gradient-estimators-for-open-institutions.md).

## Parallel ownership and finite next slice

- Open generation and shared native campaign integration: free source, wiring,
  explicit probabilities, durable proposal/evaluate/update and cold restart.
- Estimator kernel: plain LR and a mathematically explicit prior-frozen control
  variate with tractable expectation correction; analytical unit checks.
- Operational representations: keep existing native tensor/response paths;
  expand them when useful, not as a prerequisite to the other tracks.
- English evidence UI: make actual method paths and provenance inspectable;
  distinguish completed experiments from future or scripted work.
- Integration: freeze the small v11 execution protocol, audit raw evidence,
  update program status, notes and manuscript without rewriting past results.

Completing these interfaces is an engineering gate. Discovering a useful new
institution, proving estimator efficiency, improving the designer recursively,
and establishing external scientific value are empirical questions, not results
to manufacture to close a goal.
