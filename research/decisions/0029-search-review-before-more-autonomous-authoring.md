## Decision

Preserve the original researcher world and free SystemSpec/code/protocol/graph
representation. Preserve historical source pins, failures and raw artifacts.
Do not start another model/world campaign during this review.

Reuse existing ask/tell, native oracle, canonical LossSpec, mixed-design gradient
estimators, artifact reuse and cross-scoring. The LLM editor is one proposal
mechanism, not the definition or entirety of optimization. No framework rewrite,
new obligatory differentiable world or new optimizer family is needed for R0.

Separate retrospective common-pool scoring, a prospective shared initialization,
and online cross-arm data sharing. The original V44 own-history isolation was
intentional; it is not retroactively a missing-data bug. Its all-accept outcomes
cross-score -11.833333333333336 under the efficiency loss, while that arm's best
is12. This is an existing observation, not newly discovered native improvement.
Both objectives can prefer the same candidate; do not force different winners.

Retain technical nulls and exact ties. Do not add novelty, behavioral difference,
fairness or scientific benefit as an unstated acceptance gate. Different code
hashes are not proof of different behavior, and equal loss is not proof of
program equivalence. Budget/cost accounting distinguishes logical slots,
physical native worlds (including partial failures) and reused checkpoints.
