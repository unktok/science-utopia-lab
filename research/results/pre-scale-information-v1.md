The input audit establishes an operative route, not a benefit: all five first

year. M2 is not a token-matched placebo; content, length and recipient specificity
are not separately identified. Initial equality does not couple all future
fresh LLM noise, and ambiguous rendered recipient prefixes remain explicit.
