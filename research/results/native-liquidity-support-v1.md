## Interpretation and next research decision

Conserving allocation at a real native pre-cost opportunity can change activity
and publication under the low endowment, while the same program's aggregate
effect is flat under the higher endowment. This is meaningful action support
for future optimization, not an automatically invented institution or an estimate
of a population-average policy effect. The developer source, code execution,
nodes and schedules are the same within each pair; only delivery differs.
The sole reused seed61001 is development data, never held out.

The assay takes29min04.465s from writer start to closure. Four worlds produce499
completed scientist responses with444035 reported input and49595 output tokens.
The79665 cached input tokens are a subset of input. Four separately charged
scientist loads have unknown token usage. Designer-generation calls are zero.
No failed slot is refilled and no active source is patched.

The next main study still needs an important objective-conditioned question,
strong same-authority rules and a frozen unused evaluation. The observed low-base
gain aligns output and participation; it does not by itself establish different
optimal systems under those two objectives, graph-search superiority or milestone
R2. More repetitions of this four-world control are not the next research answer.
