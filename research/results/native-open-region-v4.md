## Remaining non-scale gap

The implementation is broader and better tested; **useful autonomous joint
institution/protocol/topology invention is not yet demonstrated**. Better
authoring factorization, explicit local/external references, model repair behavior
and original-world consumer adapters remain software/research issues before
population, seed or budget scale-up. The prior free-source v3 count improvement
is separate evidence, not retrospectively credited as this joint-design result.
