researchers and the approved local35B designer. Compare two explicit objectives,
throughput T and common-content utility U, and two information conditions,
scalar feedback and parent-trajectory access. Each of four own histories receives
three proposal slots. Information conditions have the same institution-editing
authority. Seed61001 is reused: this is one coupled development condition, not
twelve independent replications. Smaller loss is better.
