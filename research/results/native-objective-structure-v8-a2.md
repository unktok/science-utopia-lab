## Question and fixed experiment

The optimized object is the scientific institution, not the metric itself.
Two explicit fixed losses condition free institution code, node composition,
intermediate protocols and wiring. Original research agents, their fixed
Qwen3:8B weights, RAG, project/paper production and resource state remain the
forward world. No quality/fairness/participation veto is added. The five authors,
two-year horizon, four-year corpus and seed42 stay fixed, not scaled up.

- Throughput: `L=-A`.
- Net reviewed utility: `L=3.25*A-Q+.05*R`, where Q is the sum of actual raw
  review-score means over accepted decisions, not an institution-written score.

The two coefficients are illustrative prior-informed engineering choices, not
scientific welfare. Empty publication/review histories are legal zero loss;
reject-all is not vetoed. This makes objective incentives inspectable rather
than silently correcting them with a preferred policy.

Each objective has two generations and two conditional-IID draws per generation:
eight physical worlds maximum. Exact duplicate designs remain separate recorded
draws/worlds, not rerolled for novelty. An external self-reported Codex author
receives only own-arm outcomes and the full previous source. The generation1
utility author is a fresh specialist context, explicitly recorded rather than
misrepresented as an authenticated same designer. Thus this is an executable
objective-conditioned workflow, not a controlled causal effect of objective text
with every designer behavior held fixed. It is not an optimizer comparison.
Own completed execution traces and source are available, not only scalar loss.
The pilot is trace-informed source redesign with native loss feedback, richer
than a strictly loss-only black-box oracle. The general ask/tell interface
permits loss-only optimizers independently; no comparative efficiency is inferred.
