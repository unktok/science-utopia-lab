## Meaning and cost

The fixed rubric evaluates what title, abstract and topics support. The judge
does not receive author, paper ID, institution, policy, decision or original
review score. It is a separate opinion under fixed conditions, not ground truth
and not a rating of rejected contents. The historical cohort was already
observed; this is development analysis, not held-out evidence, policy reselection
or an optimizer comparison. Current and past T/E losses remain unchanged.

The actual observer cost is101340 prompt tokens and32493 output tokens,
with no unknown counts. Summed HTTP elapsed time is859.568 seconds; this is
not total project compute or a GPU speed estimate. All228 literal replies
pass their declared final-response and rating contract. No response is
refilled, salvaged, corrected or counted again through cache reuse.

The fixed rubric has usable but concentrated variation in this accepted-content
set. It can support a separately declared future objective, while its narrow
support and shared-model judgments limit interpretation. Introducing this
instrument alone does not close the main-study importance gap. The next
design must explain which institutional action and consequential outcome it
will study, then fix its own losses, controls, budget and unused evaluation.
