# Joint selection-fixed evaluation preparation

The [finite protocol](../protocols/campaigns/native-joint-fixed-evaluation-v1.md)
prepares a common unused-world evaluation of all frozen selections from three
normally audited studies. It does not launch evaluation, choose seeds or
declare publication readiness. The free parent-presentation experiment and
queued numeric comparator keep their existing frozen sources/plans unchanged.

## Fixed policy bank, not another optimization

Keep all8 common references and8 own-selection labels: original planning T/U,
parent presentation T/U by control/literal, and numeric T/U. Every original
own-only choice, tie and unavailable pool remains literal. A new joint label
does not reselect a policy, invent a missing submission or substitute a strong
reference. Only exact policy identity, apart from supported initial seed,
shares physical execution. Different whitespace, annotations or unused
parameters do not receive semantic normalization.

The [runner](../../scripts/run_native_joint_fixed_evaluation_v1.py) joins three
normal original double audits, checks identical scientific conditions/common
reference programs and the numeric bank's original planning provenance,
then binds explicit unused seeds, source and measurement. It locks all
original writer paths and preserves every physical job/null without retries.
No real seeds have been chosen. One to three explicit seeds and a48-world
absolute ceiling are supported, but are not a recommendation to run that
many worlds without a useful identified question.

## Outcomes and interpretations

The [reader](../../scripts/reports/native_joint_fixed_evaluation_v1.py) preserves
all16 logical labels on every seed, both T/U losses and each own policy minus
all8 controls. Eight predeclared fixed-policy contrasts cover presentation,
numeric-versus-prior-free and T-selected versus U-selected policies. A missing
pair stays unavailable. Shared physical jobs are explicit, not independent
samples or extra replication. No evaluation result changes selection.

The [original-source auditor](../../scripts/audit_native_joint_fixed_evaluation_v1.py)
reuses the existing physical/checkpoint/critic proof with an explicit new
schema. Cold planning-choice, institutional-consumer and full scientist-input
readers have a named joint scope. Costs retain original scientist/critic/load
calls. Double audits/costs/reports must agree without inference before claims.

Unused world seeds evaluate these fixed policies after development histories;
they are not new optimizer histories, independent critic errors, scientific
truth, an isolated design-freedom treatment or method-general superiority.
The scientific question, finite actual seed list and dispatch decision remain
to freeze after the ongoing studies are interpreted, not merely after their
software checks turn green.

## Order and evidence discipline

The joint plan carries explicit selection order. Sorted JSON does not retain
mapping insertion order, including the order of labels sharing a physical
job. New joint jobs reconstruct that order explicitly; legacy study paths and
immutable old logs are not rewritten. A serialization-roundtrip regression
and the full reader path check it. An inventory root disappearing after
preparation also blocks start instead of being mistaken for an empty unused
directory. The source-pinned native full-path check remains required before
any actual evaluation dispatch; its SCRIPTED results are software evidence.
