## What it shows

- Every declared slot, including generation and native-execution nulls. Native
  costs and losses remain unknown when their original measurement is absent.
- All literal T/U/C losses and collected K for both visible developer parents
  and all own proposals. C is U-loss plus0.1K; the coefficient is a declared
  development preference, not measured welfare or scientific truth.
- Three loss-path panels, not the two-panel historical renderer. Best own
  submitted loss and best known loss including visible parents are distinct.
  The original own-only tie-first selection must reconcile exactly; no parent
  substitution, retrospective objective change or favorable filtering.
- Source/node/edge/schedule differences from each actual parent and the anchor.
  A changed literal system is not automatically an operative effect.
- Each actual staged wrapper, original definition, full chronological step
  prefix, sealed terminal and component totals. Later source does not inherit
  an earlier artifact's test result. If a terminal is absent, preserve its
  retained failure and steps without manufacturing a final component summary.
  Recorded steps are not inferred total charged calls; transport/provider
  accounting remains in the original separate cost report.
- Every actual world's ordered full scientist request/response-choice hashes,
  with completed same-seed observed-input classes and separate failed prefixes.
  No timestamps, identities or scientific content are scrubbed.
- Original physical code receipts, actual received inputs and committed events,
  complete annual resource reconciliation and original rendered context
  presence in completed scientist requests. These are descriptive execution
  traces, not causal mediation or ablation results.
