## Exact versioned interpretation

V44 selects its implementation checkpoint after three inspection **attempts**,
not three successes. Planning, submission and recovery have precedence. The
reader uses each recorded step allowance/reserve/cap rather than assuming
16 calls in every journal. Known V45 context revisions are explicit; malformed
or unsupported metadata stops analysis rather than becoming zero mismatches.

Each row retains the recorded phase, a separate interpretation using the body
actually supplied, compact/spaced byte counts, inspection attempts/successes,
visibility flag/hash consistency and exact loss arithmetic/definition matching.
Only the compact-fit/spaced-over-cap general-versus-implementation case receives
the specific serializer-mismatch label. Other contradictions remain separate.
Omitted bodies have unknown byte counts, never zero. A pending intent has an
unknown response/action/change, not a failed step or zero cost.

The interpretation is based on the saved source request journal, not a newly
verified actual provider wire, measured token capacity, model understanding or
causal benefit from the prospective correction. It does not recompute native
outcomes or certify the whole interface bug-free.
