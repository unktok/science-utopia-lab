# Planning choices institutional consumers and complete model inputs

The free planning T/U study needs more than a score table. These readouts
separate submitted code, committed effects, information actually received by
the fixed researcher model, literal choices, saved native projects/resources
and final losses. They describe observed histories; they do not identify a
causal mediator or decide which institution should be adopted.

## Three original record readers

The [choice trace](../../scripts/reports/native_planning_choice_trace_v1.py)
retains every audited physical world, including failed prefixes. It reads
saved annual project dates, original direction-request frames, committed
replies, literal memories and catalog-validated assignments. Complete worlds
must have all four annual boundaries; partial work never receives a completed
loss. Unknown/malformed replies and ambiguous actor matches remain explicit.
Missing reply files cannot hide committed responses.

The only marker-presence diagnostic checks the known developer agenda string.
An invented institution need not keep that marker, preserve the advice rule
or choose the shortest topic. Its absence is not evidence that no information
institution exists or that no message reached an actor. General authored
messages are instead covered by the actual consumer trace below.

The [institutional effect trace](../../scripts/reports/native_institution_effect_trace_v1.py)
adds an explicit planning-study scope without changing earlier scopes. It
joins source-defined nodes, physical code calls and committed events, retaining
ambiguous attribution. It reconciles actual native resource transfers and
annual founder flags. The original source's model-facing message formatter
checks actual completed researcher inputs rather than arbitrary serialized
envelope substrings. Content presence is not unique recipient attribution,
LLM interpretation or evidence of benefit.

The [full-input reader](../../scripts/reports/native_observed_request_classes_v1.py)
adds the same explicit audited schema. It hashes complete ordered payloads,
including messages and generation settings, and keeps response choices and
full provider envelopes separate. It does not erase scores, identities,
timestamps or other scientific content to force equivalence. Only complete
worlds at the same seed can share an observed-input class; failed prefixes
stay listed, not pooled into complete histories.

## Cold execution and closure

Choice and effect CLIs run against the frozen scientific source's decoder,
parser and consumer implementation. Reader source/adapter hashes are retained
separately. No scientific code, model or raw record is rewritten. The
[three continuations](../../scripts/finish_native_planning_mechanism_readouts_v1.py)
wait for normal matching original double audits/costs and export each reader
twice, requiring all output bytes to match. They do not retry a failed stage,
dispatch inference or resume the stopped first attempt.

The original14-world SCRIPTED integration is extended to call all three real
reader CLIs in cold subprocesses twice after native execution and matching
audits. It is committed before that source-bound check. Literal fixture tests
also exercise partial worlds, ambiguous memories, missing wires, changed
catalogs and each continuation scope. The first invariance fixture uses a
JSON clone of an integer-keyed analyst map and fails because keys become
strings; a type-preserving deep copy corrects the fixture, not the reader or
its guards. The actual integration result and actual reader launches remain
to be recorded. Software fixtures are not scientific outcomes.
