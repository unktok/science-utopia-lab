# Accepted content contributions to the planning objectives

The two actual objectives count different properties of the same realized
accepted records: T is minus their number, while U is minus the sum of each
fixed three-critic mean minus3. This analysis separates positive, zero and
negative contributions, so agreement or conflict between recorded objectives
can be interpreted alongside the institution and project traces.

## What the analysis preserves

Every accepted decision occurrence remains, including repeated papers or
contents. For each complete world the reader checks original counts, pooled
scores and operational U, then retains the original per-record opinions,
content hashes and signed components. A shared content hash must have the
same fixed panel across worlds. Failed worlds keep missing losses and no
invented zero contribution; all unavailable own slots remain in the report.

Rational arithmetic explains the three-position means exactly, but does not
replace the floating-point losses used by the optimizer. Preference ties,
conflicts and observed minima use those literal original losses, grouped by
world seed. Rejected or unproduced content is not rated by this reader.

A zero contribution increases the accepted count without increasing recorded
utility; a negative contribution moves the two recorded sums in opposite
directions. This is arithmetic conditional on realized records, not evidence
that deleting a decision would preserve all other research behavior. The
components do not identify causal mediation, global policy optima or scientific
truth, and do not change any loss or selection.
