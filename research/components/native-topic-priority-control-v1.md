restricted comparison rather than assuming the lexical tie-break is optimal.

## Actual implementation

`utopia/system/native_topic_priority_controls.py` accepts a literal retained
core-plus-related project comparator and a sparse finite numeric topic-priority
mapping. Its actual source consumer chooses minimum original duration, then
maximum declared priority, then lexical topic. It cannot add an ineligible topic
or shorten a native project. Missing priorities mean zero; invalid topics,
booleans, nonnumeric or nonfinite weights are rejected without clipping/repair.
Integers are not silently rounded through float conversion.

Only the agenda node's parameters/source/label change. Mechanisms, ports,
permissions, wires, schedules, actors, native effects and resource laws remain
literal. Zero weights recover the original complete **component output**.
If priority changes a chosen topic, its reason annotation accurately names the
priority rule; unchanged choices retain their original annotation. Full native
scientist-input equivalence at zero weights is not yet verified.

This is explicitly a hand-defined restricted parameter family, not a restriction
on the general optimizer. Free-system authors remain permitted to replace the
algorithm, create institutions and change graph/process structure under their
declared native authority. Neither shortest duration nor positive gain becomes
a scientific validity or admission rule. No loss or metric is changed.
