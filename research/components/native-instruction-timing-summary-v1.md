## What the analysis preserves

Each request keeps its original wire index, year, instruction-presence flag,
chosen topic, original catalog duration and actor-attribution status. A missing
reply or catalog match remains unknown. Repeated requests remain attempts,
not independent choices; committed direction memories are counted separately.
The table includes all attempts and distinguishes the denominator of known
durations from all requests. An allowed related topic absent from the displayed
table is not treated as invalid or disobedient.

The choice figure places attempts at their actual native year. Multiple
attempts for one attributed actor/year share a comma-separated cell rather
than being jittered into invented years. Unattributed attempts retain their
own row. Actor display labels are local to each control and do not imply
cross-world identity matching. Stored project states/resources remain in the
numeric summary, with their annual rather than within-year resolution.

T/U values and signed differences from cut are descriptive. This one reused
seed and three developer controls do not estimate optimizer superiority or
an independently replicated mediation effect. Advice reaching a model input,
shorter topic selection and improved completed output remain different facts.
