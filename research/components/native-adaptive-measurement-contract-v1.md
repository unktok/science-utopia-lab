An operator-declared observation instrument now connects the original researcher
world to loss evaluation and the next institution proposal. The researcher
weights and native activity remain unchanged. This permits optimizing a new
declared metric rather than only reporting it after an existing search.

`NativeAdaptiveDesignCampaign.advance` accepts a trusted `measurement_adapter`
and its `measurement_contract`. The contract must already match the campaign's
frozen world binding. Missing, undeclared, changed or non-callable instruments
are rejected before generation or world dispatch. Candidate JSON cannot provide
the callable. With no declaration the historical evaluation path is unchanged.

The adapter preserves the original measurement identity and base metrics. Its
additional values enter the declared loss; the resulting observation enters the
next proposal history and persists across reopening the campaign. Every retained
measurement carries the orchestration contract hash, checked again during cold
reopening. This identifies the intended instrument, not the accuracy of its
opinions or authenticity of its raw responses; real studies must audit those
responses separately. An unknown critic response stops further dispatch without
inventing a loss or replacing a slot.
