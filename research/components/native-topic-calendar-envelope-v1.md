# Conditional calendar headroom, not a replacement researcher world

This read-only analysis asks how much paper-count headroom remains in the
normally closed two-hand-rule pilot. It does not run an optimizer, change the
active v6 study, contact an LLM or replace native scientific evaluation by an
arithmetic world.

For an unchanged original author set, let H be the horizon, P the maximum new
papers per mature project and d_a the minimum original eligible topic duration
for author a. Under sequential nonoverlapping projects starting no earlier
than year1, at most floor(H/d_a) projects can mature per author. Thus the
conditional optimistic new-paper capacity is P times their sum. Funding,
inactivity, collaboration, corpus availability and inference errors can reduce
realized production; they cannot make this arithmetic a feasible-policy solver.

The calculation additionally assumes no pre-existing output and no repeated
acceptance of the same paper increasing the accepted-decision count. It is a
conditional calendar envelope, **not a certified global bound on arbitrary v6
programs**, a complete formal verification of the trusted runtime, or a limit
on the general engine. Core-only is the developer's source rule, not the shared
action permission. No duration-only causal effect is identified.

## Implications after the running finite study, not new active conditions

Keep all six current slots and their objective/selection rules unchanged.
Interpret ties against actual reachable consumers and calendar headroom,
without requiring new nodes, different winners or improvement. Before scaling
up a later paper-count method comparison, determine whether its permitted
class already has a known saturating rule and whether a proposed effect can
change original production or another declared objective. Do not repeatedly
scale an unchanged saturated comparison, weaken the control, retroactively
add a new loss, or force an apparent discovery. A main-track claim still needs
