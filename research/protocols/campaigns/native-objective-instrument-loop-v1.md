## Question and hypotheses

When the same designer can change scientific institutions, how does replacing
`T=-A` with `U=-sum(three-critic content mean - 3)` change its proposed rules,
information/resource routes, selected systems and persistent consequences?
U uses `native-common-content-utility-v1.json` and the existing fixed observer.
The center3 is its prior anchored rubric, not a coefficient fitted to make
particular institutions win. There is no review-cost, fairness, participation
or novelty term in either objective.

Different objectives need not select different systems. If observed optima
coincide, report that and examine the action/metric support. A candidate may
beat its starting point but lose to a simple reference; that remains a failed
strong-control comparison, not invented institutional superiority. New graph
syntax, critic opinions or this finite one-history experiment alone do not
establish an important main-conference result.
