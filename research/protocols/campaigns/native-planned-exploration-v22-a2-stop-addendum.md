# Adaptive diagnostic stop after the first closed a2 arm

This resource decision is made during the live a2 trial, after its first
authoring generation but before its first closed two-generation arm. It is
not a preregistered stopping rule and does not replace the frozen a2 source,
four-slot authorization or its original protocol. Do not present the stopped
trial as completion of all four scheduled slots or a controlled comparison.

The first generation consumes16 known model replies: eight plan actions,
seven reads and one explicit retention, with no code edit. Summed author
adapter time is849.4107626674231s, separately from native measurement. Keep all
its actions, reasoning errors, costs and retained-parent measurements.

Let the throughput arm close both generations, including its own-result
feedback and the second original-world evaluation. Then stop this individual
writer before further model generation in the second arm if an auditable
between-arm boundary can be captured. Do not interrupt an in-flight model or
erase a partial journal to manufacture that boundary. If the second arm has
already started authoring, leave it running to an auditable boundary instead.

Use only the known owned writer process. Record authoritative termination,
all actual work and the two remaining scheduled slots as unexecuted, not
technical null measurements or replacements. Do not rewrite queue/campaign
records or mark a queued arm complete. Independently audit the exact stopped
raw/source state twice with explicit incomplete-budget scope; the writer's
termination and the campaign's incomplete budget are distinct facts.

Inspect whether the full exploration goal is met by the actual closed loop.
If not, keep it active and continue to the separately prepared source-fixed a3
with its own fresh actual readiness. Earlier inference and native costs are
not refunded or pooled. This stop is a debugging/resource-allocation decision,
not a candidate novelty/quality/fairness/promotion gate, scientific outcome
selection or a change to the loss. No candidate is altered or silently omitted
from the observed records, including any worse/retained result.
