## Question and estimands

How do publication-count T and saved founder-activity P objectives change the
literal institutions proposed and selected under limited native annual awards?
This is end-to-end loss-conditioned design, including an objective-dependent
editing parent. It does not isolate an optimizer algorithm or graph syntax.
Neither rewiring, different winners, positive gain nor a desired social outcome
is required. No scientific-truth or fairness requirement is added to either loss.

T is the unchanged negative count of accepted decision occurrences. P is the
negative sum of year-end can_author AND is_active flags for the actual initial
author cohort, years1--4. New actors cannot inflate the cohort; absence is zero
by definition, unknown flags/missing boundaries fail measurement. P is not
person-time, useful research, resource sufficiency or a constraint on T. Native
zero-resource flags remain unchanged and appear in outside ledger diagnostics.
