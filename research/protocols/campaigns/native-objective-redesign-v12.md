# V12: objective-conditioned free institutional redesign, before scale-up

Prospective protocol,2026-11-02, before any v12 actual researcher inference.
The root records a clean source pin in each immutable run plan before execution.
This is a NEW campaign, not an extension or rewrite of v11's closed budget.

## Scientific question and fixed objectives

How do different declared losses condition executable institutional redesign,
and what paths and cross-objective profiles result? The searched object is the
scientific organizing system. Whole-world differentiability is not a goal or
gate. Useful invention and successful improvement are empirical questions.

Reuse the EXACT existing LossSpecs, in canonical order:

1. `native-joint-v5-throughput`: minimize `-A`.
2. `native-joint-v5-net-reviewed-utility`: minimize `3.25 A - Q + .05 R`.

A is native accepted decisions; Q is the sum of ORIGINAL integer-parsed native
accepted-paper reviewer means, not arbitrary institution-authored final scores;
R is native review records. These illustrative proxies are not validated
science-policy goals. No hidden quality/fairness/novelty/promotion veto, merit
threshold, structural novelty reward or mandatory institution is introduced.

## Shared world, finite budget and methods

- Original native five fixed-Qwen3:8B authors, two years, world seed42 and the
  same four-year real-paper corpus, serialized local inference. No researcher
  weight training or simplified replacement science dynamics.
- Main redesign: TWO objective arms, THREE sequential generations and ONE
  design draw per generation: SIX planned native attempts maximum. Same initial
  SystemSpec/world settings; different exact objectives. No shared/replayed
  researcher replies. These are development executions, not independent seed
  replications or a causal objective comparison.
- Main method is derivative-free external loss-conditioned code/graph redesign,
  using `NativeAdaptiveDesignCampaign(mode='blackbox')` and one explicit external
  design strategy. It is not a claimed Bayesian optimizer or a likelihood model
  for source invention. Factors/continuous coordinates may be empty.
- Separate followup: at most TWO new native draws from v11's final saved
  corrected-control proposal, optimizer RNGstream43 and world seed42. It is a
  forward-only test with a new run/protocol/source binding, not a continuation
  of old v11 raw files. No further policy update is needed. Its parent vocabulary,
  final weights, audit and all unchanged native-runtime blobs must be verified.
  No variance reduction or method superiority follows from two observations.
- Combined budget is EIGHT native attempts maximum. Invalid-generation slots
  consume their planned authoring slot but execute zero worlds. Failed draws
  retain null losses; no replacements, hidden rerolls or success-conditioned
  extras. Later scheduled generations may respond to preceding failure.

## Free authoring boundary and provenance

Each main generation asks for a complete exact-request-bound mixed vocabulary.
New sandboxed Python source, algorithms, protocols, nodes/ports, wires,
schedules and dependency/guard/repetition processes can be authored; existing
evaluation/publication/funding mechanisms may be removed/replaced while keeping
explicit executable downstream contracts. Native activity and open-domain
capabilities connect to real researcher/project/resource/context state. Privileged
host code, arbitrary world writes or a replacement numeric science world are
not allowed. The host supplies no missing institutional semantics or repairs.

Generation0 is externally authored without v12 observations. Later generations
receive only their own preceding requests, source, draws, failures and actual
losses, including an exact incumbent and history hash. An unchanged institution
is legal; do not force arbitrary novelty or invent an improvement claim. Record
why an algorithm/process was changed or retained. External Codex authorship is
self-reported, not authenticated Qwen source invention. Technical static/native
SCRIPTED QA is labelled separately and is not additional Qwen evidence.

Actual new source/protocol/graph/process execution must be demonstrated with
receipts, not inferred from renamed IDs, new hashes or decorative animation.
No outcome-selected alternative code may replace an executed unsuccessful draw.
Any needed pre-inference authoring repair preserves the original draft/error
and is explicitly logged; consumed definitions are immutable.

## Analysis and publication

After the budget closes, independently re-extract checkpoint measurements and
each loss, replay campaign decisions and preserve raw inventories unchanged.
Keep every observed draw, failure, exact best-loss tie, source and graph lineage.
Cross-score every completed native checkpoint under BOTH losses. Cross-scores
reuse the same development worlds; they are not held-out evaluations, extra
inference or a second optimization objective. A reporting metric does not become
a search gate. Display current observation separately from running incumbent.

The English UI and paper figures must bind the exact audited report/source,
show actual changes/flows/costs and distinguish measured events from visual
choreography. Awaited/fixture data must never be shown as actual results. Paper,
notes and final source/artifact verification retain negative or null outcomes.
No expansion in people, years, seed count or other empirical budget is implicit.
