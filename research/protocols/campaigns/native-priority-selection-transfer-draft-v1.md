## Question and unit

Do the twelve objective-conditioned own selections from the mixed-method
development comparison retain their measured behavior and relative losses in
new native world-seed conditions, against the same three disclosed controls?
This tests transfer of these fixed programs, not a newly improved designer,
independent repetitions of search, a different corpus or better real science.
The original persistent four-year/five-founder world remains; no toy surrogate.

The primary record is each physical policy/world-seed outcome and its cross-
scores under unchanged U/C/T loss specifications. U is the existing fixed-model
abstract-content opinion, K collected native cost; unpaid/requested resources,
participation and other native metrics remain diagnostics, not new vetoes.
Critic positions, author turns, papers, labels sharing a policy and optimizer
histories on the same environment are not independent environment replicates.

## Bank, labels and identity (implemented pure preparation)

`scripts/native_priority_fixed_bank_v1.py` reuses the complete closed-data
projection to check every original slot/world and frozen earliest own tie.
It retains three ordered reference labels and twelve own-selection labels
(three methods × two optimizer histories × two objectives). It copies exact
submitted programs and the original selected IDs/ties/losses; it does not
compute a different best program or add a parent fallback. An absent own
selection retains its label and null program. Its future contrast is unavailable,
not zero or a forced reference-policy score. All originals remain archived.

Only exact literal policy identity modulo the single supported
`initial_state.seed` field can share a physical job. Identical development
losses, ASTs or observed full scientist inputs are not grounds for sharing.
Retain every label on a shared job and report physical/logical counts separately.
The pure draft binds all source/plan/audit/selection hashes, losses and labels.
Its `dispatchable` and `seed_usage_validated` fields are false. Do not feed it
directly to a native runner or mistake a seeded job list for unused evidence.

## Fixed analysis (not an extra scientific adoption constraint)

1. Display all fifteen logical labels, both seeds, physical IDs, original own
   selections/ties, literal program hashes, all cross-scores and technical nulls.
   Show raw U, K, unpaid/requested resource cost and accepted decisions as
   separate quantities. A C gain caused by collection avoidance is a C gain,
   not evidence of lower requested work or a reason to reject the candidate.
2. For each method/history, display U-selected and C-selected programs under
   **both** evaluation losses. At fixed K their ordering can coincide; neither
   a tradeoff nor different selected systems is required. Do not invent a
   separability threshold, drop tied programs or choose a favorable evaluator.
3. For every available own label, show its seedwise difference from **each**
   predeclared control, including the known terminal control. The development
   parent chosen under the assigned loss is a fixed additional annotation.
   Never select a new strongest reference on evaluation data and then pretend
   it was the optimizer's parent. Missing pairs remain unavailable.
4. For each objective/history/seed, show the three pairwise method contrasts
   (uniform/adaptive, uniform/free, adaptive/free), including ties and failures.
   These are paired fixed-policy outcomes, not an isolated causal effect of
   representation or equal cost. The methods have different search interfaces.
5. Show every development step and its observed own/best-own/best-known path
   separately from the frozen evaluation endpoints. Recovered known parents,
   adverse edits and runtime nulls stay visible. Do not pool author steps as
   independent evidence of learning or use evaluation feedback in a new search.
6. Trace literal edits → actual native effects → observed scientist input/choice
   classes → resources/production/content → assigned loss. Existing complete
   path equality is a diagnostic, not global program equivalence. A changed
   wiring diagram alone is neither actuation nor useful invention.

With two candidate world conditions, report all paired values and descriptive
summaries rather than impressive-looking p-values/bootstrap intervals or a
general method ranking. Any stronger claim requires an explicitly justified
independent design, not counting critic positions or shared labels as samples.

## Implementation/verification and research decision still open

The pure bank/job draft has unit and saved-SCRIPTED-data checks. The actual
outer preparation, lock/no-redispatch runner, original-origin/native-source
attestation, seed coverage, cold audit, role-cost/readout adapters and public
end-to-end software proof are **not** completed by that helper. Build and check
them before a separately declared real dispatch. Defer independent heavy
Docker/native/PDF work until the active real inference has ended.

Inspect the completed pilot first: a flat or technically failed result does
not become an important contribution merely by running these seeds. A useful
changed program motivates a targeted mechanism intervention and transfer;
flat outcomes motivate a documented question/observability/world review, not
weaker references or automatic scale-up. These are research-design decisions,
not hidden candidate-merit constraints. R2/R3/R5/R6 and publication readiness
stay open until substantive claims and evidence warrant a final submission.
