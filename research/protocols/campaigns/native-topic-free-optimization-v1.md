## Research question and losses

Can a fixed local designer construct useful executable scientific systems under
different explicit objectives, when supplied strong same-authority parents and
their measured outcomes, rather than repeatedly editing a saturated accept-all
selector? The proposal is free source/node/wiring/process search in the original
persistent researcher world. A new node is neither required nor evidence of
usefulness by itself. Preference reversal, improvement and new wiring are
possible outcomes, not submission gates or guaranteed findings.

Three losses, all minimized, are fixed before new free proposals:

- Original throughput T=-A, retaining its exact LossSpec.
- Original content-opinion U-loss=-U, retaining its exact LossSpec and observer.
- New resource-aware content C=-U+0.1K, in native units. U is the original fixed
  panel's centered abstract-based opinion, not scientific truth; K is actual
  collected annual research, production/submission, resubmission and funding
  application cost from complete reconciled ledgers. Income and conserving
  transfers are not consumption. Uncollected activity costs remain a separate
  diagnostic. Unknown inputs produce unknown loss, not zero or a merit penalty.

The coefficient0.1 is an explicit developer preference: ten resource units
trade against one centered opinion unit. It is not a measured social shadow
price, science-policy recommendation or newly discovered metric. Prior
development resource/outcome diagnostics were already observed when choosing
this study; this is disclosed, not a blind hypothesis. The coefficient is not
retuned after authoring or to force a different winner. Report T/U/K separately
for every completed system, plus all nulls. No welfare/fairness/activity/novelty/
improvement veto is added. Elementary allocation and accept/reject incentives
must be discussed, not sold as novel institutional discoveries.
