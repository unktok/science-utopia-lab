# Objective conditioned free planning and allocation pilot

This prospective pilot asks what institutions a free source/graph search
submits and selects when optimizing accepted output T versus the existing
fixed-content opinion utility U, with actual research planning authority in
the original persistent scientist world. It follows the closed three-control
catalog comparison, not a relabelled continuation of its hand-written policies.
Those controls improved both T and P on one reused seed; they did not establish
objective-specific design or generated improvement.

## World and loss definitions

Keep the original five initial university researchers, other native agents,
fixed installed Qwen3-8B weights,103107-record2016--2019 corpus and four native
years. Preserve base30 balanced resources, two-output project cap, fixed
funding fraction0.2, native costs, calendar, production and accounting. Reuse
development world seed61001 explicitly. No prior trial becomes a fresh score.

Reuse T exactly as minus accepted decisions. Reuse the existing U LossSpec
and common-content observer: minus the sum of three fixed-model critics'

input fields, score anchors, pooling, cache keys, model and generation settings

scientific truth. All accepted contents are rated under the same contract
regardless of the assigned optimization objective. Fresh measurements are
shared as reference observations within this study, not imported from old T/P
or T/U runs. P and resource/project histories are outside diagnostics, not
objectives, constraints or promotion vetoes here.

## Design freedom and strong common references

Use the named fixed-native-production-catalog-planning-v3 scope. Arbitrary
institutional source, nodes, protocols, wiring and institutional schedules
may change while original production remains protected. Catalog assignment
can choose any actual displayed expertise topic with its exact native content
and duration for an active idle author. It cannot shorten ongoing work or
grant unrestricted native.project. Review removal and all-accept/all-reject
selection remain legal. A new node, wire, different winner or positive gain
is never required. This limited study scope is not the limit of the broader
open scientific-system framework.

system on that basis. No reference is called an invention or theoretical optimum.

## Verification and following decision

Before dispatch require a clean pinned source, exact closed original catalog
audits/raw inventory, unchanged historical component support, successful
SCRIPTED full-path execution through calibration/authoring, original native
worlds, actual observer public API, outer replay and cold audits. A parser-only
or hand-copy test is insufficient. A separate original-source closure path
must reconstruct every physical world and critic, own history/selection and
all costs twice without new inference.

This is one development history per objective, not independent optimizer
replication or held-out evaluation. Close every slot and compare every strong
reference, cross-loss and actual mechanism before defining a separate finite
unused-condition evaluation and independent histories. Gains over cut alone,
hand-rule effects or a larger number of valid programs cannot establish
method superiority. Ties or nulls do not authorize repeated slots or a new
loss chosen to manufacture separation. The important main-paper answer and
publication-ready judgment remain to be established by the evidence.
