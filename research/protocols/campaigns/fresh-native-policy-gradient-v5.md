# Fresh native-cost policy-gradient pilot v5

Registered 2026-11-01 before the first fresh world.

The forward system is the original native researcher simulator, not an offline
cost bank or a learned numerical world. Fixed Qwen3:8b researchers respond anew
to the selected complete scientific system. Two exact full SystemSpecs are
transferred from the sealed v3 source campaign: its initial baseline and its
final Qwen-authored selection-code revision. Historical costs are not training
observations. This pilot optimizes their categorical selection probabilities,
not their source code, topology, researcher weights or native transition law.

## Frozen budget and loss

- One categorical REINFORCE batch, two independent-with-replacement actions.
- Initial logits `[0,0]`, search seed42, SGD learning rate0.1.
- Fixed independent baseline0.0; minimize negative actual accepted decisions
  from `native-accepted-count-engineering-v1.json`.
- Every action executes a distinct fresh complete world, native seed1301,
  two years, five independent balanced researchers, four-year cached corpus.
- Repeated actions remain separate executions. No redraws to obtain different
  actions, nonzero gradients, favorable outcomes or a new best.
- Technical failure receives no numerical penalty and prevents the whole batch
  update. It is not silently removed from the estimator.
- All model HTTP requests acquire the shared native inference flock. The code
  executes from a separate clean, commit-pinned experiment worktree.

## Verification and limits

Durable campaign state records sampled RNG/actions/log probabilities and native
observations before updating. Reopening replays the exact score update and binds
every completed checkpoint to source/model/corpus/design/native seed. A separate
read-only audit re-extracts all six native domain measurements and the loss from
portable checkpoints, checks response journal hashes/bindings and executed exact
source/image receipts, then compares Torch autograd with the closed-form
categorical log-probability gradient. All raw hashes must stay unchanged.

No pathwise derivative through the native world or LLM is claimed. A one-batch
stochastic gradient does not establish convergence, causal scientific quality,
held-out improvement, useful new institutions or topology invention. Negative
acceptance count is explicitly a gameable engineering objective; no hidden merit
or fairness veto is added. The archive-cost bank remains separate evidence.
