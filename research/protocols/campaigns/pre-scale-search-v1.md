different declared objectives, and what systems and native behaviors result?
This is not a powered method-ranking study or a requirement that new systems
win. Keep five original balanced researchers, fixed Qwen3-8B, installed approved
local35B designer,2016–2019 corpus/retrieval, four native years,768 scientist
output tokens, thinking off and serialized inference. `plan.json` binds actual
model digests, backend, corpus, runtime, source and protocol before dispatch.

The common editable initial is the developer-authored M1 history institution,
without the old example's direct project reassignment. Methods are direct
six-coordinate LLM numeric, staged LLM fixed-graph code, staged LLM code/graph,
and independent uniform sampling of those same coordinates. They share the
same numeric bounds and actual consumers, not necessarily the same proposal
interface. This end-to-end comparison does not isolate intrinsic expressivity.

The **explicit pilot domain** fixes original production/calendar/accounting
native nodes and their schedules, actual base-plan origins and amendment
authority, and the pinned code image. It rejects duplicate/wrapped copies of
those native effects or new `native.project` grants. New information/code/

everything, and no novelty, quality, fairness or loss-improvement veto exists.
This limited domain is not the global SystemSpec ceiling or a relabeling of
historical project-amending results. Enforcement is opt-in `world_law_scope`,
validated before any candidate world and again on cold campaign validation.
