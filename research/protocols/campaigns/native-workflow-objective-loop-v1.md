# Native objective-conditioned workflow search v1

Prospective finite development experiment. This protocol does not modify any
running presentation/numeric study, dispatch a model itself, or promise gain.

## Question and scope

Can loss-conditioned local design exploit workflow/wiring freedom in the
original persistent scientist world, beyond original R7 and a strong explicit
timing control? Source-only edits in the closed pilot did not beat strong
rules; its calendar was fixed. This new condition makes workflow authority
real, not an author's unused parameter or a toy surrogate world.

Within this experiment, all original R7 mechanisms, sources, numeric parameters,
ports, state and seed remain fixed. The designer may change any annual phase
and remove/add any connections among existing typed ports, subject to the
explicit workflow-v4 technical domain. This **structural-only author interface**
does not replace the general code/node/whole-SystemSpec framework. It cannot
establish a controlled causal effect of design freedom relative to an older
experiment whose input interface, source and proposal count differ.

Original scientist population, native production/cost/grant/attrition rules,
four years, corpus identity, Qwen3-8B identity, two-output/project cap and
annual one-slot funding condition are preserved. Native effects execute once;
original phases are not shortened or mocked in scientific execution. A later
change in eligibility or actual charged costs is a possible *outcome* of
timing, not a guarantee of equal realized flows or real scientific benefit.

## Losses and controls

Keep the original T=-accepted decision occurrences and U=-sum of fixed
three-critic mean scores centered at3. Keep both cross-scores and native
diagnostics. These proxies are not scientific truth. There is no added
participation/fairness/quality/novelty/improvement veto or penalty for technical
failure. T/U need not prefer different systems in this small world.

Two fresh shared references use the exact R7 implementation:
R7-original and the explicitly hand-designed post-funding-cost control.
The latter changes three phases/five edges with literal sources/parameters.
The previous eight-reference experiment remains evidence in its own scope,
not fresh outcomes or an allegedly equivalent baseline bank here.

Each objective has three own slots. Parent selection is minimum assigned loss
from completed fresh common references plus that objective's own completed
history, earliest ties in chronological bank order. Final selection is earliest
minimum **own** policy, with all ties; no reference substituted if no own policy
completes. Best-known incumbent and own-output curves must remain separate.

## Readout and claims

calibration, campaign definition, checkpoint, effect witness, scientist load,
observer score and selection; preserve all nulls and actual costs. Compare
all generated graphs with parents and controls, not just fingerprints or loss.
No static validation or SCRIPTED HTTP test counts as scientific gain.

Interpret native timing effects against actual resource ledgers, attrition and
researcher input changes. If benefit relies on an inherited simulator edge
case, report that boundary explicitly; do not call it improved real science.

unused evaluation. Structural invention, stable superiority, a general
optimizer effect, world-level backpropagation and publication readiness are
not established by successful execution or this finite pilot alone.
