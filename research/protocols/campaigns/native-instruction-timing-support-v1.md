## Grounding in the original implementation

utopia/agents/research_direction.py:build_direction_prompt lists each eligible
direction's Expected Project Duration. ResearchDirectionSelection has topic,
detailed_focus and reason fields, **no free duration field**. The selected
topic is resolved through DIRECTIONS_DICT to the original direction/duration.
Therefore the control may advise choosing a listed short-duration topic; it
must not write a new duration, overwrite a project or change production laws.
Choosing a shorter listed direction need not maximize output: expertise,
conference availability, funding, later choices and retrieved contents matter.
No benefit is assumed. This grounded distinction prevents testing an
instruction aimed at a nonexistent native action.
