## Motivation and implementation change

The original designer repeatedly targets nonexistent or wrong array-element
fields and sometimes confuses source strings with numeric coordinates/schema
objects. These are low-level authoring-interface errors, not useful-invention
or scientific-value conditions. Implement a general stable-mechanism-ID edit
IR with explicit local field pointers, an unrestricted full-document patch
escape, deterministic compilation and structured pointer/type diagnostics.
Expose exact current path/type catalogues and all own prior detailed technical
failures. Literal source/schema/operation values are not guessed or repaired;
missing/ambiguous targets or branch-dependent mappings fail explicitly.
Record original IR, translated atomic edits and compiler/protocol identities.

The legacy direct authoring constructor and a1 code/receipts remain replayable
from their frozen source. A new clean source pin is mandatory for this QA;
never edit the historical experiment checkout or relabel a1 drafts as valid.
