01 / THE RESEARCH QUESTION

Optimize the system.
Not just the scientist.

What we ask science to achieve may change the institutions we should build. We turn that question into something we can execute, observe and optimize.

This research fork contains and extends the original ScienceUtopia researcher world. Peer review is one example—not the boundary. Evaluation, publication, funding, governance, and their connections and processes are design objects.

The optimization loss, technical execution validity, independent evaluation metrics, and optional adoption policy stay separate. Fairness or novelty requirements are not silently added to the objective.

02 / WHAT WE BUILT

From a concept
to an executable platform.

01

An extensible representation of the researcher world

IMPLEMENTED

Typed state, nodes, ports, wires, schedules and processes connect to the original lifecycle and real consumers. The authority boundary is explicit: naming a new node does not magically implement arbitrary native activities or treasury powers.

02

Institutions as executable designs

IMPLEMENTED

An LLM designer can edit code and configuration, probe components, submit a system and receive measured native-world losses. A source edit, an actual change in researcher inputs, and an objective improvement are three different observations.

03

Objective-conditioned closed-loop search

IMPLEMENTED

Numeric and free code/graph search use measured ask/tell feedback. Finite budgets, strong references, execution nulls, frozen selections and audit trails are retained. An operational optimizer is not evidence that it invents a better institution.

04

Gradient information where available

SCOPED

Scoped tensor representations, relaxations and expectation-gradient estimators complement black-box evaluation. This is not universal autograd through arbitrary LLM calls and code, nor evidence of general live-world gradient superiority. Differentiability is a tool, not the research goal.

05

Inspectable records and a game-like interface

IMPLEMENTED

An English pixel/cyberpunk interface exposes events, institutional source, graphs and loss histories. Canonical evidence stays distinct from presentation-only choreography. It is a working replay/design/observation interface—not a finished management game.

FINDING 01

Edits did not guarantee changed behavior.

Of eight free-design proposals, six complete candidates repeated a measured parent's full researcher-input path; one changed inputs and worsened utility; one failed execution. No wire or schedule edits were generated.

FINDING 02

Improving a proxy is not improving science.

A separate hand-designed control improved the declared C loss by 4 while preserving utility: collected resources fell 200→160, unpaid costs rose 0→40, and requested expense stayed 200. This is neither automatic invention nor less requested work.

What the quantities mean

U is a sum of centered abstract-panel opinions, not scientific truth. K is collected native activity resources, not compute, money or real work.

05 / WHERE WE STAND

What exists.
What remains open.

What we can share

  • Executable institutional designs and composition interfaces.
  • Objective-conditioned optimization loops in the native researcher world.
  • Experiments with controls, nulls, loss paths, behavior and cost records.
  • A game-like observation UI, concept figure, manuscript and code.

What we have not shown

  • Useful autonomous invention and stable gains over strong simple rules.
  • Unused-condition evaluation of the latest corrected frozen selections.
  • Universal world autograd or general gradient-method superiority.
  • Recursive improvement of the inventor, real-science benefits, or main-track readiness.
