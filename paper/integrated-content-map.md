## Non-negotiable distinctions

Do not pool historical sources into a matched experiment. V8/V12/V13 external
authoring is not the local35B V2 proposer. Different utility coefficients remain
different losses. Same-checkpoint cross-scores, shared prefixes, controller
histories and shared policies do not create independent worlds. No new model
call, source execution, causal intervention or inferred total project compute
is added by this manuscript. Old final-decision receipts remain historical;
an expanded argument is not new experimental evidence or conference readiness.
