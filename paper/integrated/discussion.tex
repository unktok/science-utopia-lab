\section{Discussion: what can be concluded across the program}
\subsection{The objective-to-system map is the research object}
The studies are not another survey of hand-selected acceptance or funding
settings. A loss enters a search procedure, which returns a specification and
a history; common evaluation then characterizes that returned system.
Parameter optimization exhibits two selected designs under three objectives.
Structural cases exercise different organizing algorithms. V2 shows that
strong simple rules can remove apparent gains against an editable seed.
The meaningful output is the conditional map from objective and permitted
design domain to generated mechanisms, paths, costs and consequences.

That map need not be injective: different objectives can select the same
institution. It need not be robust: a development winner can change rank on
unused conditions. It need not select a novel institution: all-accept or
reviewer-free reject may minimize a proxy. These are valid experimental
outcomes, but not automatically important general findings. A future paper
must substantiate which observed pattern extends beyond elementary incentives
and this particular designer/world combination.

\subsection{Expressivity, exercise and optimization must be measured separately}
The completed controls in Section~\ref{sec:native-workflow-controls} do exercise
different timing and information routes with consequential native outcomes.
They are hand controls in a stopped experiment, not own optimized programs.
The same-grant resource contrast is useful mechanism evidence, but it does
not cure the missing automatic objective-to-institution result. Likewise,
directed thinking-mode calibration succeeds while free proposals reach an
output limit or violate graph causality. Platform expressivity, a compiler-valid
submission, an operated change, a loss improvement and independent validation
remain separate claims.

The later open-code T0 in Section~\ref{sec:native-workflow-feedback} actually
changes subsequent native scientist inputs and selected content, while tying
parent throughput and worsening the fixed common-content loss. Changed source
and exercised information flow therefore do not by themselves imply a useful
institution. Its following calibration stop does not erase that unfavorable
observation or complete the missing objective-conditioned search.

The V8/V12 cases show that source-defined nodes, routes and plan processes can
execute; V13 shows observed free-code gains with an external author. The later
matched local study does not exercise declared topology changes at all.
These are compatible results with different provenance, not a contradiction
to be resolved by discarding the earlier cases or attributing them to the
local designer. The platform can support a behavior that a particular search
does not find within its finite budget.

The subsequent actual staged study in Section~\ref{sec:native-staged-construction}
completes every own slot but still does not add nodes or wires. One agenda
program changes native scientist inputs and worsens utility, while the other
five own outcomes tie R7. Component observations, successful submission and
whole-world completion therefore cannot substitute for an identified benefit.
Unequal inline objective-definition coverage is an observed presentation
limitation, not a demonstrated causal explanation. Future information/authority
comparisons must not be described as repairs of this closed experiment.

Section~\ref{sec:native-planning-search} strengthens that distinction. All six
local proposals execute, but only rewrite an already all-accept selector.
The permitted planning and allocation freedom is not exercised. Complete
inputs show three U programs preserve their strong parent's observed behavior,
while two T programs change inputs and outside U despite equal T. The realized
content objective refines count ties without strict preference conflict; all
accepted content contributions happen to be nonnegative. A lack of generated
advantage here cannot establish that no better institution exists, nor can
different objective-conditioned outputs establish an unavoidable trade-off.

Section~\ref{sec:native-reserve-response} examines an allocation parameter's
actual consumer without mistaking conditional commands for new worlds. Its
240 component evaluations produce nonmonotone transfer responses on four
fixed historical inputs, while the original ledger connects one real command
path to cost, award and attrition timing. This explains an exercised operation
and motivates a separate process-design question; it neither supplies a
counterfactual loss frontier nor establishes a useful newly invented system.

The finite numeric comparator in Section~\ref{sec:native-numeric-comparator}
then completes all six native worlds. Its own U path improves while its
reference-inclusive incumbent does not; its T proposals all remain worse than
the known reference. This is why trajectories must distinguish best-known
from best-generated outcomes. The cross-study comparison to the earlier
free-program pilot is post-hoc and not an isolated effect of design freedom.

The concrete agenda comparison in Section~\ref{sec:native-catalog} supplies
a useful planning path, rather than just an unconsumed interface: ten advice
choices match their actual agendas, and direct assignment separately validates
ten native effects. Both effectful arms raise accepted decisions from 12 to 18
on one reused seed while improving P as well. This is developer intervention
evidence, not a locally generated institution or a distinction between the
systems selected by different objectives. Its later full model-input histories
differ, so it does not isolate duration as the sole causal mediator.

Similarly, technical validity rates are scientifically relevant to end-to-end
optimization, but they confound any claim about intrinsic design-space value.
In V2, all local LLM numeric proposals failing while uniform numeric proposals
execute is evidence about the tested package. It is not proof that parameters
are intrinsically harder than code or that code/graph freedom is superior.
Future interface interventions should be prospective, not repairs/refills of
the V2 record.

The original-choice controls in Section~\ref{sec:native-choice-resource} extend
this distinction: advice is delivered and output improves relative to
unadvised choice, yet direct assignment remains stronger. None of the twenty
native direction requests chooses a one-year topic despite an unrestricted
topic-string schema. This does not identify why researchers choose as they do,
nor justify calling a hand-authored information office an autonomous discovery.
The new passive ledger instrument exposes a finite output--resource contrast
that T/U alone did not express. Retrospective cost measurement and fixed-pair
loss algebra are not a new optimization study, an independent replication or
a reason to replace the old objectives after seeing their outcomes.

The same-authority topic contrast in Section~\ref{sec:native-topic-system-search}
subsequently improves both observed losses at equal collected cost by changing
a hand rule's candidate set, without shortening original topic durations.
It identifies an actionable developer contrast, not a useful automatically
generated institution or a duration-only causal effect. Its conditional calendar
relaxation is saturated on the recorded fixed founders/support, while every
accepted content contribution is nonnegative; neither diagnostic proves a
world-level optimum. The separate three-objective free search uses both strong
references visibly. All six own slots close: an observed-path-inert tensor
edit, source reproducing the weaker known core rule, an ineligible two-hop
assignment, an AST-preserving submission, explicit parent retention and
accept-all code with a conditional empty-case incompatibility.
Five native outcomes complete, one is null and none improves the strong
parent. U's own best improves by returning to a known rule, not by moving the
reference-inclusive incumbent. These cases and their paired reconstruction
separate available freedom, exercised actions and actual loss without
identifying a general causal explanation. Adding a resource
preference is a declared new study, not retrospective reinterpretation of old
T/U or their selections. The separate terminal-cost study in
Section~\ref{sec:native-terminal-cost} now realizes a four-unit $L_C$ gain:
identical recorded scientist inputs and accepted content accompany 40 fewer
collected units and 40 more unpaid units. This source-only hand rule establishes
reachable objective opportunity, not local-designer discovery, lower requested
expense or better science. New non-veto component diagnostics remain software
preparation, not evidence that they improve free-system search.

\subsection{Gradients are an option, not an ontological requirement}
The most general target is free scientific-system optimization using available
evaluation and gradient information. Exact autograd is appropriate inside a
represented differentiable institution; response surrogates require native
fidelity checks; likelihood-ratio estimation can surround a discrete/opaque
world if its sampled design distribution is known. No universal differentiable
replacement of language, retrieval and science is required.

Our measurements do not show a practical estimator advantage. The V9 fitted
operator fails an unused finite-path check, V10's moving methods tie the known
incumbent, V11's updates do not discover a better first best, and neither V13
gradient followup improves its previous best. These observations should guide
better estimator targets and uncertainty accounting, not be hidden behind
the fact that a backward pass exists. They also do not prove all gradient
methods ineffective: targets, information and tiny budgets differ.

\subsection{From institutional revision to recursive improvement}
There are at least four different claims: a program is revised; the revised
program has a better observed loss; repeated revision improves expected
performance on new conditions; the revision procedure or inventor itself
becomes better. Our external-author cases sometimes support the second, and
local pipelines implement the first. They do not establish the third or fourth.
Scientist/designer weights are not trained here. A history-aware author is
not thereby a recursively self-improving optimizer.

A persistent society that changes its own organizing system is another axis.
It raises state migration, policy-selection bias and counterfactual evaluation
questions not answered by resetting worlds. Checkpointed designs, explicit
adoption and source-bound trajectories provide infrastructure, not a completed
recursive-improvement result. Keeping these levels explicit preserves the
long-term motivation without selling it as current evidence.

\section{Related work}
ScienceUtopia studies a persistent research ecosystem and institutional
interventions~\citep{jin2026scienceutopia}. Our design object is the executable
organizing system returned by a loss-conditioned search. This is a different
question, not a claim that the source world is realistic enough to validate
policy or that its reported large-model experiments are reproduced locally.
Science-of-science and research-assessment work motivate plural objectives
and skepticism about proxy optimization~\citep{fortunato2018science,hicks2015leiden}.

Programmatic mechanism design already synthesizes multi-agent transition and
reward components in a domain-specific language, combining stochastic program
search with Bayesian parameter optimization~\citep{narayanaswami2022programmatic}.
Thus neither representing institutions as programs nor separating structural
and numeric search originates here. Our setting asks how such search behaves
when persistent, fixed-weight language-model researchers interact with
scientific institutions; that application is not itself a new synthesis
algorithm or evidence of greater generality.

Optimization through generated programs is prior art. OPRO uses evaluated
solutions as feedback~\citep{yang2024opro}; ADAS searches code-defined agentic
workflows~\citep{hu2024adas}; AMD-LLM evolves mechanism code with problem-specific
fixing~\citep{liu2025mechanism}. Our no-host-repair contract is a comparison
choice, not a superiority claim. SGA combines LLM outer expression search with
continuous inner optimization~\citep{ma2024sga}. GPTSwarm optimizes agent-graph
connections with score-function methods~\citep{zhuge2024gptswarm}. Thus code
generation, graph search and mixing discrete design with gradients are not
first contributions here.

FunSearch couples code generation to an evaluated program pool
\citep{romeraparedes2023funsearch}. AlphaEvolve samples prior programs and uses
an evolutionary database for exploration and exploitation
\citep{novikov2025alphaevolve}. ShinkaEvolve studies parent/inspiration sampling
and novelty rejection \citep{lange2025shinkaevolve}. Retaining our complete
history is not implementing all these search policies, and our local pilots
are not benchmark comparisons against them. We do not import a novelty veto
into the assigned institutional objective or infer that these methods transfer
successfully to our world. A prospective method comparison must hold authority
and information fixed, retain nulls and report actual computational costs.
Neither an evaluated archive nor ordinary feedback-driven code search supplies
our missing scientific-system finding by itself.

Behavioral collapse and design cost are also not new observations:
\citet{el2025inefficiencies} compare archive-curation strategies, evaluation-
vector similarity and economic viability in code-defined question-answering
agents. Our exact native input/choice classes are a different diagnostic in
a persistent world, not a priority claim or evidence that our search learns
from history. Those results do not establish the cause of our local stagnation.

AFlow already searches code-represented workflows with Monte Carlo tree
search and execution feedback~\citep{zhang2024aflow}. ADAPT for Science evolves
the optimization procedure as well as where to search, evaluated on unseen
physical designs~\citep{feng2026adapt}. Mining the Design Space of Multi-Agent
Workflows retains intermediate workflows and tests mined design principles
on held-out searches~\citep{cadet2026mining}. Thus retaining trajectories,
using errors as feedback or revising an optimizer is not a standalone novelty
claim. The AI Scientific Community also precedes our broad vision of
interacting virtual laboratories, while describing its working implementation
as under development~\citep{braganeto2026community}. Our remaining empirical
question concerns which observations, actions and decision times matter when
an objective selects an institution in a persistent researcher world; the
current pilots do not yet supply a general answer.

SciMARS adapts scientific multi-agent harnesses through separate role and
coordination editors, with an additional memory-policy editor in its extended
variant~\citep{wu2026scimars}. Candidate executions inform composition and
selection; revision is task-local and the outer optimizer remains fixed.
Scientific harness adaptation, component-specific editing and evidence-aware
revision therefore are not first contributions here. The longitudinal-information
comparison being run in this program varies which native parent trajectories
reach an institution author, not the researchers' memory service. Its results
are not reported here before the frozen analysis closes. It is neither a reproduction nor a matched
performance comparison of SciMARS. The candidate distinction is optimization
of the organizing system of a persistent researcher society under declared
losses; that setting alone does not establish an important empirical answer.

More direct institutional precedents also narrow the novelty claim. MACC
proposes a scientific blackboard with reproducibility rewards and an
adaptable, potentially differentiable incentive mechanism; it is a Blue Sky
architecture and research agenda~\citep{oyama2026macc}. A Society of Researchers
describes persistent laboratories, competitive funding and independent review,
while leaving matched organizational comparisons open~\citep{asaria2026society}.
Institutional AI specifies executable governance manifests, policy programs,
notices and replayable logs for multi-agent markets; its fixed-topology policy
screening precedes locked independent evaluation~\citep{bracale2026institutional}.
An executable institution artifact or screened social-policy selection is
therefore not new here. The inspected implementation does not establish free
LLM-authored topology/code search, but that scoped distinction is not a field-wide
priority claim or a reproduction of its empirical results.
The Station executes open-ended agent research in persistent rooms with
scorable tasks~\citep{chung2025station}; EurekAgent studies permissions,
artifacts and budgets for executable discovery~\citep{xin2026eurekagent}.
Neither an institutional vision nor adding verified computational tasks alone
is new. Our empirical contribution must be the objective-to-institution
relationship and its consequences, not priority for these general ideas.

The AI Economist studies learned social policy~\citep{zheng2020economist}, and
LLM Economist adapts workers and a planner through context under bounded tax
actions~\citep{karten2025economist}. AgentSociety 2 integrates composable
environments and specification-to-code~\citep{piao2026agentsociety2};
SocialSystemArena compares governance specifications~\citep{fei2026institutions};
PolicySim optimizes exposure actions in an LLM-based social
environment~\citep{huang2026policysim}. We focus on editable scientific
organization and its longitudinal native effects. Domain specificity alone
does not establish greater generality, scientific significance or priority.

Likelihood-ratio, reparameterization and control-variate methods are existing
theory~\citep{parmas2021unified,parmas2022proppo,parmas2023composite}.
Our contribution is the explicit placement and evaluation of these boundaries
in this design environment, not a new unbiased estimator for arbitrary
LLM-generated institutions.

\section{Limitations and responsible interpretation}
The native world is itself simplified. Literature retrieval stands in for
many real scientific activities; it does not conduct physical experiments,
verify novel knowledge or reproduce the full social organization of science.
Our extension preserves that world but does not eliminate its assumptions.
The native action registry is extensible, not an already implemented semantics
for every possible laboratory, government or hypothesis-testing institution.

Experiments are small and exploratory. Historical objectives and coefficients
are engineering choices informed by development, not preregistered hypotheses
about real science. The parameter study's five evaluation seeds and V2's five
unused seeds are small; their source/design spaces differ. Other cases share
one development seed, some use common-prefix replay, and external-author
provenance differs from local 35B generation. These distinctions prevent pooled
causal method rankings or broad model generalization.
The new information comparison has three four-year development seeds; its
single acceptance and variable paired differences are descriptive, not a
reliable mechanism ranking or an objective-conditioned search result.
The separate closed T/E pilot has only two proposals per arm, one development
seed and three unused evaluation seeds. It completes objective-conditioned
selection and comparison, not a reliable method ranking. Its five free-source
runtime failures and absence of actual rewiring remain part of the result.

Review scores are endogenous LLM outputs, not truth. Better count, review
utility or resource snapshots do not validate scientific value, equity or
harm. Proxy-seeking institutions should be examined, not deployed by default.
Section~\ref{sec:design-diagnostics} makes the observer dependence explicit:
unchanged measurement code does not imply institution-independent panels.
Its fixed-record readouts neither change actual review work nor establish
that any submitted institution manipulated its panel successfully.
The later routing-authority campaign stops before its planned evaluation;
its separately completed thirty-world fixed-submission evaluation has a
different protocol and retains both unavailable graph arms. Its numerical T
selection ties accept-all, and numerical E loses to one-reviewer accept-all
on all three seeds. Section~\ref{sec:native-loop} also shows that
conditional proxy incentives can coincide and that an earlier completed
cohort's saved activity stock is flat. Neither establishes a global optimum
or an absence of transient resource pressure.
The subsequent count/content development closes eight submissions with six
completions and two technical nulls. Its selected programs tie strong rules;
four candidates have a reference's identical observed model inputs, while
other tied scores accompany changed inputs. Its separately closed eighteen-world
fixed evaluation retains all three unused seeds: T-selected ties connected
accept-all rules on publication count, while its cross-objective U differences
against no-review accept-all are mixed. U-selected is literal reference
retention. More completed world seeds do not turn one design history into
independent optimizer replication or a stable strong-rule advantage.
The three advice-timing controls in Section~\ref{sec:native-advice} establish
literal message delivery without a topic/duration or T/U gain at their reused
seed. They do not establish complete behavioral equivalence: full requests,
replies and some resource trajectories differ. These developer contrasts are
not new optimizer histories or evidence against information institutions in
general. The separately closed capacity pilot in
Section~\ref{sec:native-capacity} submits four more programs, with three
completions and one invalid virtual-source transfer. All complete programs
tie strong reserve allocation rules in both T and P without changing wiring.
Its two observed score points have no trade-off under any nonnegative linear
mixture. This describes the finite archive, not all reachable institutions:
three complete submissions do not distinguish objective alignment from a
designer that preserves an incumbent's behavior. Its prepared unused-seed
evaluation is not dispatched merely to enlarge that flat comparison.
The later three-arm planning comparison changes actual topic/duration choices
but remains a single reused-seed developer intervention; it supplies neither
independent optimizer histories nor a held-out benefit claim. The subsequent
free planning pilot in Section~\ref{sec:native-planning-search} is normally
closed, but its own assigned losses tie its best references. The separate
structural compiler-feedback search and its unused evaluation also close;
their selected systems retain the hand's observed behavior. The open-code
pilot instead stops after one own completion. These different closure and
information conditions must not be pooled as independent method comparisons.
No study of actual researchers, human intervention, independent truth verifier
or real scientific-policy deployment is reported.

Reproducibility has layers: numerical reconstruction from retained reports,
full raw/source re-audit, and fresh inference. The first is demonstrated for
V2, the new closed information study and the T/E pilot, with explicit historical receipts
for other studies; fresh inference is not
guaranteed by seeds. The local 35B upstream revision/conversion recipe remains
unverified. Some usage is unknown and total project FLOPs, energy and monetary
cost are not measured. Appendix~\ref{app:reproduction} states these boundaries.

This manuscript uses a retained NeurIPS 2025 preprint style for readability,
without a page cap; it is not compliance with a selected future conference
cycle. Human authorship/responsibility and public redistribution rights are
not finalized. Experimental LLM roles and AI assistance in implementation,
analysis, figures and prose must be disclosed, not represented as independent
human peer review. No actual submission, public upload, model replacement
is performed by preparing this manuscript. The new source-specific readiness
and information-path study have their own explicit protocols and current
statuses in Section~\ref{sec:prescale}; they do not amend historical experiments.

\section{Conclusion}
The system that organizes science can be an executable optimization object
containing the original persistent researcher world. Institution code,
mechanisms, connections and processes are not restricted to a menu of
acceptance/funding knobs, and black-box evaluation can coexist with scoped
gradient information without making the entire world differentiable.

The integrated evidence is mixed and informative: objective-conditioned
parameter selection yields uncertain trade-offs; structural programs can alter
real plans and information routes; external source redesign can improve an
observed proxy while gradient followups worsen; the matched local study
reveals compliance failures, no exercised topology search and no advantage
over strong simple references. A canonical game-like interface makes those
same successes, ties and failures inspectable.

The completed four-year T/E pilot adds an observed numerical feedback-history
decrease and selection-fixed evaluation on three unused seeds. It exercises
source algorithms but not new wiring; the small paired difference from reviewed
accept-all does not establish superiority. These results target organizing
freedom's native effects separately from interface burden and elementary proxy
incentives, not scale alone. The program separates implemented capability,
exercised mechanism, observed optimization and stronger claims still requiring
evidence.

The closed authoring and panel diagnostics sharpen the remaining boundary:
static submission is not full native execution, offered explanation is not
received explanation, and an endogenous score is not independent knowledge.
These are explicit experimental distinctions, not new optimizer superiority
or a substitute for an important institutional-design result.

The separate thirty-world evaluation preserves the stopped search rather than
completing it retrospectively. Numerical T ties accept-all's throughput;
numerical E loses to a one-reviewer rule on each tested seed, and no working
graph policy exists for evaluation. These outcomes motivate distinguishing
the objective's incentives, available authority and actual program execution
before treating larger-scale search as the missing ingredient.

Planning authority makes the distinction concrete. Advice and direct native
assignment increase output under a fixed developer agenda, but the following
six-proposal free search only rewrites strong all-accept selection code.
Its two objectives have overlapping observed minima, and its content instrument
supplies no negative accepted contribution. Full-input comparisons separate
actual behavior retention from equal scalar scores. Useful autonomous invention
and general optimizer advantage remain open; a larger run alone need not
resolve narrow exploration.
