\subsection{A same-authority developer contrast in the original world}
\paragraph{An interface distinction, not a new research law.}
The original unforced researcher can choose core or directly related topics,
whereas the earlier typed direct-assignment action permits only core expertise.
This mismatch is local to the historical fixed-effect design domain: the
general engine already has a broader project API, excluded there because it
also permits arbitrary durations and ongoing-project changes. It would be
incorrect to conclude that the platform cannot express related-topic projects.
A new explicit action exposes the original eligible union while preserving
the original catalog contents, durations, idle-researcher preconditions and
actual native project consumer. Historical actions and domains stay unchanged.

In a separate completed pilot, both developer systems receive the same new
action permission and the same source, graph, schedule and resource authority.
Both use the hand-written shortest-original-duration rule with lexical topic
tie-breaking; only candidate membership differs: core-only versus core plus
directly related topics. This rule belongs to each submitted institution, not
to a host-enforced search restriction. The four-year, five-founder society,
103,107-paper corpus, fixed Qwen3-8B scientists and original T/U observer remain.
The reused seed 61001 is development data, not an independent replication.
Two original-source cold audits verify equal initial physical societies and
48 original native-effect witnesses per world. No designer generates a policy.

\paragraph{Actual calendar, content and resource paths.}
Core-only assigns ten two-year projects. The broader rule assigns sixteen
one-year and two two-year projects, all eighteen outside core expertise; no
original duration is shortened. Accepted year/paper/content multisets share
zero records, with twenty core-only and thirty-six broader-only occurrences.
The exact utility difference is $40/3$, not the same twenty records plus
sixteen more. The first full scientist request already differs at index 0 in
year 1, so this contrast does not isolate duration-only mediation.

Each original annual ledger collects 50 research-resource units, for total
$K=200$ in each world. Collected production, resubmission and application costs,
and separate uncollected activity costs, are all zero here. Equal $K$ is not
equal inference compute, monetary expenditure, research quality or welfare.
Both original objectives prefer the same hand rule; the experiment supplies
no preference reversal or global optimum.

\paragraph{Conditional calendar headroom.}
A read-only relaxation of the completed trajectories asks how many new paper
slots the original calendar permits, not how a new scientist would behave.
For fixed founder $i$, let $d_i$ be the minimum original duration in that
institution's permitted candidate set, $H=4$ the horizon and $P=2$ the native
paper slots per completed project. Under sequential projects, no initial
project and no repeated acceptance, the calendar relaxation is
\begin{equation}
 B_{\mathrm{calendar}}=P\sum_{i=1}^{5}\left\lfloor H/d_i\right\rfloor.
\end{equation}
Core-only has durations $(2,2,2,2,2)$ and bound 20; directly related topics
give $(1,1,2,1,1)$ and bound 36. Both observed acceptance counts reach their
respective relaxation. This is conditional saturation of a fixed calendar,
not a global bound for arbitrary institution-workflow designs: changing
actors, effects or support invalidates these assumptions. The relaxation
also ignores funding, retrieval failures and content generation and supplies
no utility or resource-cost bound. Core-only is a submitted rule's restriction,
not the broader shared authority of the two institutions.

\paragraph{Fixed content support and tied objectives.}
All 56 accepted occurrences in these two recorded worlds have nonnegative
centered observer utility: the core pool has 15 positive and 5 zero records,
and the related pool 30 positive and 6 zero records. Their exact rating sums
are $46/3$ and $86/3$; original floating loss values are retained rather than
replaced by a differently rounded rational conversion. If one only filters
each already generated fixed pool, accepting all records maximizes count and
utility; zero contributions yield respectively $2^5$ and $2^6$ utility-optimal
subsets. These are combinatorial subsets, not 32 or 64 native worlds. Removing
an acceptance in a real world can change later funding, activity and content,
so this diagnostic is neither a deployable oracle nor a world-level optimum.
At fixed $K$, the new resource-aware objective below orders these systems just
as utility does; a new objective is not required to produce a different winner.

The original pilot closes in 31m13.34s on the shared host, with 306 completed
scientist responses (127/179),168 separate common-observer POSTs and two
scientist role-load attempts. Scientist usage totals 458,048 tokens:
428,849 prompt and 29,199 completion; cached 68,876 prompt tokens are a subset.
Critic usage is separately 78,177 prompt and 24,728 output tokens. Load usage
is unknown, not zero. There are zero designer-generation POSTs. Matching
paired cold audits/costs and all three readout files reconstruct the original
records; they do not replicate inference or establish an exclusive machine.

\subsection{Free-system optimization under three declared losses}
\paragraph{Purpose and prospective boundary.}
The hand-rule gain motivates a stronger visible starting bank, not another
weak-comparator claim. A separately frozen study evaluates two fresh
references and schedules six own proposals: two each for original throughput,
original content-opinion utility and a prospective resource-aware content loss,
\begin{equation}
 L_T=-A,\qquad L_U=-U,\qquad L_C=-U+0.1K=L_U+0.1K.
 \label{eq:native-topic-three-losses}
\end{equation}
Here $U$ is the original centered fixed-panel opinion, not truth. The coefficient
is a developer preference exchanging ten native resource units against one
opinion unit; it is not an estimated social value. Earlier resource diagnostics
were observed when choosing this new objective, which is disclosed. The new
loss is fixed before these free proposals and never substituted into historical
selection. Missing cost or outcome remains unknown. No fairness, activity,
novelty, improvement or promotion veto is added.

\paragraph{A free organizing system, within an explicit native-effect scope.}
The new institution-workflow domain permits authored Python, new mechanisms,
ports, protocols, wiring, schedules and bounded internal processes. It also
permits institutions to assign typed work to existing fixed-model original
researchers and consume their response artifacts. Exact tasks, responses and
canonical receipts are recorded. Work does not itself publish papers, assign
review scores, overwrite ongoing projects, mint resources or train weights.
The original native research, production, funding and resource effects remain
fixed; historical domains are not silently widened. This finite study's
once-per-year outer invocation is not a universal engine limitation.

Both developer references are visible in full to every objective's author.
The current minimum-loss measured reference or own system is the parent.
Only explicit own completed submissions enter the final own selection; a
missing proposal is not replaced by a reference. Unchanged-parent submissions
are legal and labelled. The order is T,U,C,C,U,T, with optimizer seed 68011;
these histories are not independent optimizer replications.

The approved local 35B designer uses sessions bounded to 16 model calls each, with
8192 output tokens and 64k context. Fresh source/model/backend/request-bound
readiness precedes initial references and each proposal. Directed-control
success is not free-design capacity or invention evidence. Optional component
probes return actual errors to later turns but are not a finish veto and do
not trigger host code repair. Twenty original developer inputs are development
fixtures, not fresh worlds or held-out evidence. Scientist models remain
Qwen3-8B, with all additional work counted against the same 1024 HTTP-attempt
bound per world. The new activity loss does not charge LLM inference compute;
that separate per-role cost must remain visible.

The maximum is eight physical worlds, six consumed own slots, 376 designer
POSTs and 384 component probes, with finite declared transport/session/world
allowances and no failed-slot refill. Transport completion-unknown or failed,
stale or unknown readiness stops inference. Every technical null and adverse
outcome remains recorded. A prior full public software construction exercises
all eight new native worlds, six prescribed programs, twenty-four institutional
work calls and matching paired cold/cost reconstruction, but HTTP, corpus,
critics and child supervision are SCRIPTED. It establishes neither real-model
capacity nor autonomous discovery.
