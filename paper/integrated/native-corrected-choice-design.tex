\subsection{Actual proposal spaces and reference information}
Three references are freshly measured: the literal zero-priority policy,
its known terminal-consolidation version and the literal core-plus-related
policy. Only the first two are visible selectable references. Every method
starts from the first minimum complete visible reference under its assigned
loss. An old measurement is not assigned to a new program or source binding;
reference technical failure stops rather than substituting a fallback.
The supplied terminal rule's accounting effect is not autonomous invention.

Both restricted methods retain the same 53 original topic-priority fields in
$[-1,1]$, with minimum original duration first, then decreasing priority and
lexical topic. All other fields remain literal. Uniform sampling uses the
existing numeric sampler throughout. Adaptive search uses the same first
sample and then a declared rank kernel: choose uniformly among fixed initial
actors' nontrivial shortest-topic groups, swap the group's current highest
ranked topic with a uniformly selected alternative, and encode the resulting
global order with binary-exact bounded priorities. At scale $s$ and $G$ groups,
the move performs $\max(1,\lfloor sG\rfloor)$ swaps, with the existing cooling
schedule initialized at 0.2. The groups come from the fresh reference's exact
year-one pre-agenda actor projection and original catalog/eligibility, not
favorable measured losses, new rollouts or a behavior-change filter. Repeated
groups, undo and repeated observed behavior remain possible. This conditional
initial neighborhood does not exhaust later native policies or guarantee loss
locality. The rank move is not new optimizer theory.
Existing exact-program uniqueness checks remain; they do not reject repeated
observed scientific trajectories.
The internal sampler index can consequently exceed the number of evaluated
own slots: duplicate-vector draws create no additional native evaluation.
They are part of the declared proposal algorithm, not scientific failure
retries or replacement slots.

The seed alone does not define a common random-number intervention across
objectives. The sampler also binds the full numeric space and parent program;
objective-dependent reference selection can therefore change its RNG namespace.
Within an objective, the uniform/adaptive initial literal programs are paired.
Separate physical executions of that pair are not independent policy samples.

Free proposals retain arbitrary executable source, mechanisms, protocols,
ports, connections, schedules and bounded process composition under the same
v6 native effect contracts. They need not stay inside the priority family or
add a particular node. Their greedy measured-parent procedure retains actual
own observations and complete declared loss/history information; a source
change, explanation or successful probe is not a measured native gain.
Retaining history does not mean repeating every historical raw body in every
model message: compact views and explicit on-demand record descriptors are
part of the interface, not proof of model understanding.

\subsection{Technical readiness is not downstream scientific success}
The prospective visibility correction uses one literal document-presence
decision for display and workflow phase. Historical requests remain unchanged.
Each actual free request needs its own fresh source/model/backend/request-bound
readiness certificate; prior assay certificates are not transplanted. These
technical checks are distinct from native loss evaluation and useful design.

The component option explicitly projects the current node's non-reserved
parameters onto attributed recorded inputs, state, period, phase and randomness.
It uses the existing isolated executor and labels the derived context as
counterfactual. It does not simulate a new candidate trajectory, change historical
fixtures or establish the applicability of inputs after new wiring/state.
Probe errors do not veto explicit submission, and the host does not repair
source or implement the author's prose. Local code return and compatibility
with a later native consumer remain separate properties.

Before this real dispatch, a clean source-bound public/native software check
executes all 24 own attempts and three references, including a prescribed
execution null, through actual SDK/container/checkpoint/paired cold/cost/readout
paths. HTTP replies, corpus and synchronous orchestration are scripted. Its
351-file native source closure matches the scientific source, with separately
checked support helpers. This is engineering evidence, not new real-model
capacity, scientific improvement or outer subprocess-CLI verification.

\subsection{Charged slots, endpoints and interpretation}
The native budget remains 1,024 scientist HTTP attempts per world, a 900-second
read allowance with zero retries, 7,200 safe-point wall seconds per world and
6,000 fixed-observer POSTs. The designer ceiling is nine readiness sessions of
at most 40 POSTs plus eight proposal sessions of at most 16 POSTs: 488 combined
POSTs. Component probes are capped separately at 512; role loads are separate.
Shared-host wall time is not a dedicated throughput benchmark, and equal
proposal counts do not imply equal compute.

A slot is charged and journaled before dispatch, not when its result becomes
available. Known generation/execution/measurement failures remain terminal
nulls with unavailable loss, never artificial penalties, free retries or
replacement candidates. A pending response is not an observed failure or a
completed world; uncertainty does not authorize redispatch. Observation, actual
optimizer tell and final selection are separately retained.

For method $m$ and target $j$, the primary endpoint is the first minimum-loss
complete policy in the ordered pool of visible measured references followed
by chronological own proposals. Its origin remains explicitly reference or
own. The secondary endpoint is the first minimum complete own proposal, or
absent when none completes. The primary is not a disguised own fallback;
the secondary never acquires a fabricated parent-policy label. Observed-own,
best-own and best-known trajectories are separate, so recovery from a poor own
sample is not confused with a gain beyond an already measured reference.

All 24 slots, nulls, literal programs, cross-losses, effects, complete model
input/choice classes and role costs must survive original paired analyses.
Equal observed paths are not global program equivalence or independent
replication. Both endpoint banks must be frozen before a separately declared
selection-fixed evaluation with strict unused-condition inventories; proposed
seeds alone are not evidence of unusedness. No such evaluation or full closure
is supplied by this methods section. After complete traces, any additional
research contrast needs its own finite design rather than automatic scaling
of a flat result. A consequential differentiated claim, adequate independent
evidence and a final main-track judgment remain open.
