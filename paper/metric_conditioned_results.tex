\section{Metric-Conditioned Optimization Study}
\label{sec:metric-conditioned-results}

The preregistered study holds the design space, optimizer, budget, and seeds
fixed while changing only the optimization loss. It uses four-year
longitudinal Qwen3-8B worlds, five candidates per objective, and three common
development seeds per candidate. Each arm freezes its winner before a common
held-out suite evaluates all three objective-conditioned systems. Topic entropy
and review burden are deliberately narrow proxy objectives, not normative
policy recommendations.

\subsection{Optimization-stage results}

The optimization phase is complete. All 45 logical design--seed rows reached a
terminal state: 30 were technically valid and 15 failed at the preregistered
reviewer-capacity boundary. Failed rows retained \code{loss: null}; no numeric
penalty, protected-slice veto, or undeclared promotion constraint entered the
optimizer. Twenty-nine rows were physically executed and 16 complete rows were
adopted from byte-identical initial trajectories under a hash-verified
provenance contract. The 45 physical process attempts consumed 29.99 serial
hours.

The participation--review objective selected a different system from the other
two objectives. Topic diversity and review burden selected the same system on
the development split, while the topic objective could not distinguish the two
initial systems. This is evidence that changing the optimized metric can alter
the selected institution in this simulated setting; it is not yet evidence
that one system generalizes better. The simultaneous winner seal was written
before any held-out registry existed.
