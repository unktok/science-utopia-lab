\paragraph{What the selected program actually changes.}
The selected source replaces \texttt{world.selection} with unconditional
acceptance for the addressed paper records; its graph, schedules, and process
contracts remain unchanged. It writes a displayed final score of~10, but the
measurement adapter calculates $Q$ from the \emph{original reviewers' overall
scores}, not that field. Thus the loss gain is not a direct overwrite of the
evaluation value. Neither all-accept selection nor an increased publication
proxy establishes algorithmic novelty or better science. Its empty-input
fallback fabricates placeholder IDs and is not validated by this trajectory.
An obvious hand-written all-accept rule is therefore an essential next control,
not a result to withhold because it is simple.
